# E1 full-history confirmation binding

This additive method owns prospective E1 full scheduled histories and frozen
factor recurrence and lifetime reductions. Historical exposed records keep
their original schemas and exposure. These declarations are included in the
main extension and executable aggregates. Registration supplies factories and
codecs; scientific selection, qualification and authority remain separate.

Construct an `E1HistoryAllocation` with all four preparation families, one
explicit physical coordinate per family, fresh confirmation roles and all four
PCG64DXSM history streams. Every primary and fine state, momentum, coupling,
tick and coarse, bridge and fine innovation is retained. Fine half steps remain
in the numerical controls; factor observation times match the primary physical
clock. The allocation preserves preparation-family differences, including the
organized decreasing-family initial condition.

Supply actual outcome-blind prospective allocation and scientific-selection
publications as `E1AllocationReservation` and `E1FrozenSpecification`. The
selection binds an `E1FactorRule`, original scalar 0.30 normalization,
independent numerical and factor-witness tolerances, and an
`E1ConfirmationCriterion`. Counts, probabilities, confidence, multiplicity,
observation cadence, causal risk cutoff, duration, inference views and survival
endpoints are required choices. Presence recurrence and observed new admission
are distinct estimands. Moving and origin-fixed survival remain separate;
`BOTH` is available only when explicitly selected. Marginal cell coverage
cannot produce a full-study supported verdict.

`confirmation_draft(root=..., sources=..., scalar_definition=...,
proof_owners=..., draft_id=...)` composes six existing-runtime capabilities:
a pure commitment of the complete prior published source selection,
full native history, independently expressed numerical controls, factor
measurement, literal raw-state spectral-witness checks and a separate reveal
adjudicator. The caller supplies a complete stock `StudyDraft`, source
materializations, genuine qualification receipts and explicit obligation
owners. Stock authoring, issue, approval, source closure, execution and reveal
services remain mandatory. A candidate allocation or software fixture grants
none of those authorities. The native worker rejects unsupported contexts and
changed prior publications before contact. Every history depends on the
outcome-blind commitment's original ordinary receipt and exact output.
Public authoring and loading authenticate the exact original publication
manifests, commit markers and bytes. Staged copies retain separate physical
identities; source qualification or a native input guard alone does not
authenticate original physical custody.
Uncertain contacts are not replayed. Select finite operational task requests
within the registered resource ceiling when constructing a stock execution
envelope; resource selection does not change scientific parameters.

`science.read_retained` receives original authenticated ordinary receipts,
source, history, numerical assessment, factor product, factor assessment and
exact native and diagnostic archive bytes. It verifies receipt output hashes,
protected scopes and original operation identities, then independently
reproves all numerical transitions and the raw operator, all 15 eigenpairs,
projector, factor and scalar witnesses. It performs no native acquisition, RNG
or eigensolve. Receipt authentication and source qualification remain the
existing custody service's responsibility; this consumer does not create trust
from caller-supplied records.

`retained.read_root` performs protected custody readback through
`ExternalTaskReceiptStore` and `ExternalNativeSubworkStore`. Supply the original
authorized artifact plane, exact retained execution-plan identity, original
input manifests, four explicit phase/attempt locators, and the pure commitment's
exact attempt and original input manifests. It verifies plan
lane, run, source commit, capability and output contracts, actual ordinary and
nested receipt identities, complete prior commitment closure, manifests and original native roster before calling
the cold scientific consumer. Genuine issue, execution and reveal validation
belongs to the composing API; a selected retained plan creates no authority.

Population reduction retains all assigned independent roots and every family,
selected view and sector. Scheduled occupancy, causal admission, left-present
onset, moving and origin-fixed exits, missing identification and late-admission
censoring stay explicit. Actual admitted risk denominators remain separate
from requested-root denominators. A retained non-success ordinary receipt can
be supplied as `E1UnavailableRoot` to the cold reduction; it makes the complete
claim unevaluable and cannot supply a fake factor assessment. An authenticated
contact without a receipt is explicit `UNKNOWN_CONTACT` with no replay. An
entirely absent contact needs the platform absence census and remains a readout
refusal here. Assessed numerical and scientific
negatives retain their measurements and complete scopes.


## Declarative public facade

The installed `matrix.factor-confirmation.prospective` protocol exposes the same
full confirmation graph through `campaign experiment-explain`, `experiment-author`,
`experiment-run`, `experiment-read` and `experiment-recover`. Copy
[the specification selector](../../../../../programmes/foundations/emergent-structure/workflows/matrix-factor-confirmation/specification.json)
and supply a canonical `E1ConfirmationSelection` under the input key `selection`.
The selector's zero digest and replacement labels are deliberately nonexecutable.

The selection retains the actual stock `StudyDraft`, complete original sources,
scalar definition, explicit obligation owners, task budgets, formal entry/catalog
and source inventories, qualification receipts, source configurations and existing
resource envelope. The facade derives the six-operation-per-study graph and
canonical input census from those records; it does not synthesize a scientific
root, mark obligations satisfied, create a signer, or supply issue/reveal acts.
Absent prerequisites remain classified first stops. Select four workers through
the operator profile, with matching resource envelope, actual source snapshot and
an EL output root. Ordinary public authoring and campaign requests authenticate
current source, original publications and execution/reveal authority.

Historical E1 operation manifest/configuration bytes remain unchanged. Existing
`confirmation_draft` callers remain supported. This declaration creates no claim
that a genuine E1 confirmation has executed or qualified.


## Compact fresh-population successor

The separate `matrix.factor-confirmation.compact-prospective` protocol uses
[the compact selector](../../../../../programmes/foundations/emergent-structure/workflows/matrix-factor-confirmation/compact-specification.json)
and a canonical `E1CompactExperimentSelection` under input key `selection`.
Its `E1CompactManifest` retains the actual complete allocation/exposure,
allocation and scientific-selection publications, original factor/scalar rules,
numerical designs and observed producing provenance. Per-root configs pin that
manifest and retain one root's coordinates, four streams and selected mathematics;
they contain no complete population or package source-file census.

Version 2 `E1CompactCriterion` defines an occurrence/persistence map. Its exact
primary allowance of 1/160 is divided among eight PRIMARY-Y cells: occurrence
and joint moving/origin-fixed survival in each of the four families. Each cell
has two-sided noncoverage 1/1280 and tail probability 1/2560. X, FINE and the
separate survival endpoints have descriptive marginal 95% intervals. Nested
views and sectors do not increase the independent-root denominator.

Risk begins at zero. Initial raw factor admission counts immediately; later
admissions retain the selected causal trailing-persistence rule. The earliest
admission by `occurrence_end_tick` supplies the origin; the matching
`survival_origin_end_tick` and complete history must allow the full selected
follow-up. The programme uses occurrence by 1024 and 128-tick follow-up in a
1152-tick history. An earlier unassessable interval prevents a later observed
event from being called the first origin. A later longer episode cannot replace
the first. Initial presence, first admission after absence, return after exit,
identification loss, unobserved time and numerical nonuse remain explicit.

Every assigned root contributes to the occurrence denominator. Conditional
survival intervals take the envelope over feasible eligibility and outcome
completions; complete-case intervals are separately conditional on
assessability. No established risk gives an undefined conditional cell.
Unknowns are not observed failures. There is no minimum-risk gate or universal
probability threshold. The result uses `PARTIAL` with
`e1-estimation-map-no-binary-claim` for an observed characterisation, and
`UNEVALUABLE` when no root has numerically usable observations. Neither status
asserts a universal support claim. See the
[current E1 specification](../../../../../programmes/foundations/emergent-structure/records/matrix-e1-occurrence.md).

The original parameter-history marcher, independent numerical equations, factor
measurement and literal raw-state/eigenpair checks supply the science. The ordinary
protected service owns authoring, qualification, issue, execution, reveal and
receipt-based recovery. Public full reads authenticate current grants and original
publications, replay every completed root's independent checks without native work
or an eigensolve, and compare the exact terminal reduction and adjudication.
Partial reads defer scientific product access. The selector is deliberately
nonexecutable; it supplies no real allocation, authority or favorable result.
