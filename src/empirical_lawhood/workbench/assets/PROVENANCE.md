# Workbench brand assets

SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-4.0

These assets supply the EL blog's typeface and selected logo to the local console.
Their original bytes and source identities are recorded in `brand-provenance.json`.
Use the original licences when redistributing these files.

`el-logo.svg` is the owner-selected gentle-growth mark, selected on 24 October 2026.
It is copied unchanged from the project's retained blog brand master.
The project figure licence, CC-BY-4.0, applies to this copy.
Preserve its geometry, proportions and gold colour when scaling it.

`el-equal-circles.svg` is the separately named recursive equal-circle master
selected on 11 November 2026 for the research explorer. It is copied unchanged
from `docs/blog/logostudies/variants/i-equal-circles.svg` under CC-BY-4.0.
Outer and nested centre lines retain the same slope. Diagram arrangements are
separate interactive derivatives; ring sizes do not encode scientific progress.
The original gentle-growth master and blog assets remain unchanged.

`InterVariable.woff2` is copied unchanged from the retained blog design assets.
Its SHA-256 in `brand-provenance.json` identifies the exact font binary.
It is the Inter typeface by the Inter Project Authors.
The [upstream project](https://github.com/rsms/inter) supplies the font under SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The unmodified upstream copyright and licence text is retained in `Inter-LICENSE.txt`.
Retain that notice and licence with every distributed copy of the font.
The font is not relicensed under the project's software or figure licences.

The broker serves these assets from its fixed local resource list.
It makes no remote font or image request.
