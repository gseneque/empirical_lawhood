# Target-directed recursive inquiry policy

SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-4.0

The version-two specification, menu and policy records connect an application target to its declared
requirements and their prerequisites. A finite menu can investigate a supplier,
adopt or reuse authenticated completion, independently assess a contribution,
refine a question, wait for a missing condition, or close a specified local
question. The scientific meanings belong to the domain adapter. Shared planning
checks the joins and performs the selected ordering.

Current public commitment uses `WorkBoundInquiryState` version 2.0.0 and
`WorkBoundInquiryDecisionRequest`, decision and packet version 3.0.0.
Historical robust version-two envelopes remain byte-exact archives; they cannot
enter a new commitment or source-validated successor admission.

Use the [recursive inquiry guide](../using/recursive-inquiry.md) for
the public authoring, decision, execution and receipt-recovery route. The
[architecture](architecture.md) separates scientific requirements, task
dependencies and record lineage. These policy contracts preserve those owners.

## Freeze the question and its frontier

[Kernel policy records](../../src/empirical_lawhood/kernel/recursive_inquiry_policy.py)
retain the target, required claims, exact scoped requirements, connections,
assessment contract and complete positive, negative, unevaluable, missing,
operational-failure and uncertain-contact outcome map. Every prerequisite must
belong to the declared target graph. Routine use of a known method has supply or
assessment semantics; it does not require invented competing explanations.

[Planning policy records](../../src/empirical_lawhood/planning/recursive_inquiry_policy.py)
bind each ACT to its selected draft, configuration, input origins, capability,
complete operation count and scientific work identity. Source, scope, cutoff,
role, campaign/right, prerequisites and assessment witnesses are checked before
ranking. An assessed outcome requires its actual scoped evidence witness.
Physical or biological target claims remain unattempted until their own
independent assessment exists.

The public projector retains every actual attempt's original configuration,
registered scientific work, authored wave, execution plan/task and exact receipt
or recovery witness. The compact domain projectors omit only the operational
configuration label from work identity, retaining implementation/capability,
source, scientific design, operands, receiver, scope and cutoff. A current ACT
must match an authenticated prospective draft or registered continuation.

Procedure evidence, actual instance supply, consumer admission and successful
joined use are four separate findings. All four must be satisfied before a
requirement is credited as supplied. Cross-scope reuse needs a satisfied admission
assessment for the exact destination requirement and actual source product.
Common code, matching names or another assessment in the stock cannot supply
that compatibility finding.

## Choose and preserve uncertainty

Each policy declares criteria for target progress, further capability,
scientific discrimination and cost. Criterion bounds identify their supporting
evidence or explicit conditional design assumptions. Lexicographic and partial
ordering preserve dominated and incomparable alternatives with their reasons.
Unknown bounds and expenses stay unknown; they do not become zero. Optional
finite allowances filter the complete proposed wave, while unrestricted policies
still retain actual expense and unknowns.

Authenticated completion can be reused without novelty. Renaming an unchanged
method, operand or work identity does not authorize indistinguishable acquisition.
Every current ACT is checked against the complete actual completed-attempt census;
omitting a menu completion or changing its purpose label cannot authorize a repeat.
An explicit REUSE/ADOPT joins the original complete receipt and product denominator.
`recover_inquiry_completion` and the public decision result read that exact
authored wave and its retained products. Fresh authoring/execution of the selected
same-work reuse intent refuses. A later consumer with different scientific work
can still use an authenticated supplier through the existing contribution route.
Read-only reuse adds no scientific operation, wave or depth to finite allowances;
it preserves prior expenses and leaves unmeasured read CPU/time/memory unknown.
Uncertain contact requires custody reconciliation before the same work can enter
again. A renamed configuration cannot change that work. Unknown legacy work
blocks ACT conservatively; separately selected authentic different work preserves
the earlier uncertainty. Failed execution does not become negative scientific evidence.

Exact supported ties use stable alternative identifiers. Incomparable maximal
ACT alternatives use the declared HOLD or human-resolution policy. When no ACT
is eligible, an eligible assessed local STOP takes precedence over generic HOLD;
otherwise HOLD retains the missing prerequisite. A branch STOP leaves target
claims and other requirements open. Target STOP requires their complete assessed
denominator. Human choices require the actual actor, applicable right and reason;
revisions preserve earlier decisions, stock, source scopes, cutoffs and costs.

Target STOP requires every required target claim to be SATISFIED.
Every prerequisite reachable from the target's required requirements, including
further prerequisites at any depth, must have all four supply findings SATISFIED.
Disconnected optional requirements do not supply or prevent target completion.
A complete negative denominator supports scoped branch closure and preserves the unresolved target.

## Supported exposed adapter routes

The [material binding](../../src/empirical_lawhood/adapters/methods/analytic_material_inquiry/policy_binding.py)
and [Rubisco binding](../../src/empirical_lawhood/adapters/methods/rubisco_inquiry/policy_binding.py)
provide `policy_supply_draft`, `inquiry_specification`, `inquiry_menu`,
`input_origins` and `assessed_use_draft`. They author their fixed application
target and the exact original analytic prerequisite. Their prospective menus
name one actual known supplier or independent consumer. The disclosed counts
refer to that conditional operation and contrast; they are not probabilities,
physical benefits or competing-supplier performance estimates. Prospective cost
remains unknown. The API projects authenticated state, commits the decision and
rechecks admission at authoring, execution and recovery boundaries.

The [public service acceptance](../../tests/test_robust_inquiry_acceptance.py)
executes the source-only origin, committed supplier choice, registered PURE
supplier, independently assessed later instance, four connection findings and
revised unresolved application frontier. Material SI/slab expectations use
independent scalar equations. Rubisco expectations retain the original complete
Wilson rows and test both endpoints separately; the authentic held contrast
fails specificity. Complete custody is moved and read after removal of the test
producing root, preserving all member bytes and producing no new native contact.
Rubisco's [portable source census](../../tests/fixtures/rubisco/nomination/source-census.json)
authenticates original archived operands as exposed software-conformance inputs,
with zero fresh independent units and no execution receipts.

The same acceptance file runs only the installed battery PURE source-policy gate
over complete exposed twelve-trace diagnostic operands. Negative and unevaluable
cases retain all twelve checks and nine comparisons. The gate grants neither
numerical adequacy nor scientific authority; native qualification and later use
remain unentered. The [policy conformance cases](../../tests/test_recursive_inquiry_policy.py)
separately test battery uncertain contact, matrix factor/native utility failure,
insufficient lifetime variation, absent preparation headroom and withheld-regime
transfer failure. Changed questions get new assessment and source identities;
old results remain in their original scope. These are explicit exposed policy
cases, not completed matrix studies.

The [domain control tests](../../tests/test_rubisco_inquiry_acceptance.py) also retain
compatible conditional arithmetic, missing endpoints and wrong assembly or
reference controls. Material clock/source/receiver controls remain in its
[acceptance tests](../../tests/test_analytic_material_inquiry_acceptance.py).
Historical version-one records and draft constructors retain their original
bytes and meanings. Explicit archive reading uses
`read_inquiry_decision(revalidate_sources=False)` or
`campaign recursive-inquiry-result --historical`; it authenticates retained
members and historical joins without current eligibility or execution admission.
The original version-one ordinary inquiry protocol remains distinct.
These exposed routes establish software conformance;
physical material construction, mutant kinetics, independently qualified battery
models and the corresponding consequential targets require their domain plans.
