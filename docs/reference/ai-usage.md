# Working with AI

SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-4.0

I use AI extensively and deliberately to attempt research I could not otherwise undertake alone.
The starting ambition was dependable fusion propulsion for a journey to Neptune.
EL emerged from pursuing the scientific questions that ambition exposed.
The [About page](https://empiricallawhood.org/el-about/#is-this-ai) describes that starting point.

AI helps make the investigation possible.
The project concerns how knowledge can support further discovery and action.
Developing AI or improving AI models is not its objective.

Working this way has also produced practical lessons.
A review of 34 EL-related interventions across 17 chats examined what agents did after instructions.
Instructions often changed immediate actions, while completing the larger task remained harder.
Acknowledgement, active goals, work orders, executable prerequisites and delivered results could diverge.
All six selected stop/pause requests produced consistent stopping behaviour.
These observations describe particular working relationships and tasks, without establishing a model ranking.
