# Finite joint descriptions across twenty completed studies

SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-4.0

On 21 November 2026, twenty of the 22 re-authored preprint families had a complete primary CPU study. Across those studies, 680 calibration and 484 evaluation assignments produced 13,464 calibration and 9,612 evaluation receiver observations or explicit missing observations. Of the evaluation rows, 8,738 had measured or calculated outcomes and 874 were unsupported. Actions, horizons and receivers are nested within each root. All twenty original assessments and their six products could be read again with zero new acquisitions. The machine-readable record gives the original run, producing commit and tree, six product digests, census, receiver errors and costs for each family. [Study results and provenance](joint-description-results.json) · [Family designs and recipes](../../docs/release/preprint-recursive-migration.md)

Each study froze its candidate descriptions and selected an arm on calibration. An arm had to meet the authored coverage, all-use prediction loss, action regret and safety criteria on evaluation. Conditional mean absolute errors below refer only to supported observations. Costs in the economic comparison use the authored prices. The original workers used a total of 1,377.95 CPU-seconds. Verification work and superseded attempts have separate receipts.

## Circuits and Matrix preparations

| Family | Held-out result |
| --- | --- |
| R01 · Independent branches | Separate branch laws recovered both voltages to about 2 × 10⁻¹⁶ V mean absolute error. Retaining only the common mean gave 0.04397 V error on each branch, above the 0.020 V scale. All 96 action menus were complete. |
| R02 · Coupled ladder | The calibration-selected interface had terminal-voltage error of 0.05560 V at 0.25 s and 0.02350 V across horizons. The authored scale was 0.030 V. Its all-use normalised squared loss was 1.141 against a limit of one. Joint state and rank-eight history both passed, with the joint state cheaper under the selected prices. |
| R03 · Matrix geometry | Joint admission occurred in all 30 product cases and zero of 162 single-factor or disordered cases. The joint description reduced joint-gap error to 0.09228 from 0.27429 with the local description. |
| R04 · Conditional commitment | Eighteen of 32 held-out roots had an eligible past event. Every admitted root had commitment and occupation equal to one for every option and horizon. Fourteen other roots contributed 168 unsupported rows. All arms missed the 90% coverage requirement. |
| R05 · Approximate algebra | Twelve of 32 held-out roots admitted the algebra at the origin and retained it on all 72 tested action/horizon pairs. Twenty roots contributed 600 nonentry rows. Only 24 of 64 menus were complete. |
| R06 · Passive response | Across 15 directions, mean absolute directional error fell from 0.0008485 with the scalar description to 0.00002976 with the joint operator and 0.00001294 with operator memory. Calibration selected the scalar description under the authored prices. All three had zero paired action regret. |
| R07 · Signed response | Local curvature gave longitudinal and transverse errors of 2.25 × 10⁻⁸ and 3.73 × 10⁻⁹ in native displacement. Interface or joint information improved precision. All four arms met the workload criteria and the local arm had the lowest observed economic loss. |
| R08 · Radial increment | Local radial error was 5.33 × 10⁻⁹ at 0.032 and 9.45 × 10⁻⁸ at 0.064 in squared-radius units. All arms met the authored 0.01 scale. The local arm had zero regret across 64 menus. |
| R09 · Absolute position | The increment-only description had 0.46525 absolute-position error on the 0.1 scale. Carrying the baseline through the interface reduced error to 0.0032543 and paired regret to zero. All 192 observations were in support. |
| R10 · Forecast reuse | Local, interface and joint handoff forecasts had signed-displacement errors of 1.96 × 10⁻⁸, 2.02 × 10⁻⁹ and 1.90 × 10⁻⁹. All met the 0.00005 scale. The local description had the lowest authored economic loss. |
| R11 · Preparation reuse | Local signed-displacement error was 3.74 × 10⁻⁸. All 192 action/horizon cases met the service threshold. This cohort provided no service failures on which to measure discrimination. |
| R12 · Timestep sensitivity | All 192 paired cases agreed on service at the two selected timesteps. The largest signed-response difference was 1.51 × 10⁻⁹ and the largest handoff-position difference was 3.92 × 10⁻⁴. |

The minimal arm's conditional simulator error was 318.25 eV (root standard error 90.78 eV). All 108 simulated endpoints across both splits were independently reproduced. The two physical entry decisions retain zero acquired roots and no scientific result. The R17 and R22 numbers above use the CPU backend. Remote GPU comparison has a separate acceptance procedure. The finite comparisons give different answers for different receivers. Independent branch laws sufficed for R01. R02 needed the full ladder state or enough history at the short horizon. R09 needed the baseline even when a local increment was known. R06 gained accuracy from joint information while its priced decision still favoured the scalar arm. Limited physical action coverage in R13, R15 and R22 prevents a complete evaluation of alternative interventions on their logged cohorts. Each result retains its development scope and original producing source.
