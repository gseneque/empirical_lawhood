## Scientific question

When does knowledge of a building improve later comfort and energy decisions?

## Findings and unresolved questions

The retained building comparison measured a particular comfort–energy
trade-off in one matched office simulation. Separate heat-pump and multizone
prediction studies reached different conclusions about useful action information.
They have different tasks and denominators.

This release supplies the documentary account and retained capability boundaries.
It does not provide a public building-control study runner or the historical
producing records. Select this programme to inspect evidence and remaining
requirements, not to infer a runnable experiment from the programme's presence.
