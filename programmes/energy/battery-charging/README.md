## Scientific question

Which state and response knowledge permits a dependable charging decision as the battery changes?

## Findings and unresolved questions

Numerical continuation studies distinguish full-state restart from a
reconstruction using a few observed quantities. Reuse on another preparation
revealed different later voltages after equal-charge histories and a separate
departure from current-proportional response.

Those findings apply to the declared model and numerical views. Later thermal
and charging investigations retain precision failures, unknown completion and
unentered consumer roles. A completed local calculation does not establish a
qualified charging service. Read the [recursive results](records/recursive-prerequisite-results.md)
and the dated charging records together.
