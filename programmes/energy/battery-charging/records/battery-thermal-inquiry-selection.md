# Selected thermal inquiry after the completed battery premise

This is a separate numerical study. The current selection is the
1.1.0 conservative hybrid method. Its frozen `battery-broad02` supplier completed
all 36 development forecasts and returned an `UNEVALUABLE` numerical procedure:
all nine independent reference comparisons failed the unchanged precision gate.
Later use and reuse remain unentered. The exact-guard
[1.0.0 selection](battery-thermal-inquiry-exact-guard-v1.json) retains its original
bytes (SHA256 `f73f54c3e9505667fba05dbdea1f1cf518634f1b8c618c9f443b60f4839149e2`)
and producing attempt. That attempt is not relabeled as this numerical repair. The completed 900-second,
0.36375 Ah standard-current service at 23°C and 27°C remains unchanged. The
machine-readable [selection](battery-thermal-inquiry-selection.json) freezes the
question, complete roster, evaluator, comparisons and numerical precision.

The actual supplier used one registered native operation, 56 main charging
subsolves, 36 separate initial-SOC solver attempts and 20 tier transitions.
Thirty-five forecasts reached the 2.5 Ah target; one Chen SPMe forecast at
35°C with the 40°C chamber hit the thermal service boundary first. These
are conditional numerical outcomes within one exposed family, not physical
trials. All nine primary/refined DFN pairs exceeded both the 1 mV voltage and
0.01 K temperature tolerances; the 25°C original-ambient pair also differed
by 2.181754 s. No reduced model was selected. Provisional reduced-model
comparisons use the refined DFN, while the unresolved primary/refined gate
prevents final admission.

The completed invocation retained 206 operational members, 172,845,522 bytes,
and an exact durable copy. Its measured window through copy verification was
2,640.556216 CPU seconds and 2,689.168285 wall seconds; final writes, exit and
historical engineering/research expense remain unknown. The separately selected
[component diagnostic](battery-dfn-precision-diagnostic-selection.md) preserves
this negative result and tests localization, mesh and solver tolerance. It
cannot retrospectively qualify this supplier or authorize its unentered uses.

The new question is whether an assessed temperature-dependent model-selection
procedure preserves the admission, finite-menu charging choice and completion
forecast when thermal preparation changes. [Chen et al.](https://doi.org/10.1149/1945-7111/AB9050)
parameterized the LG M50 and validated room-temperature discharge;
[O’Regan et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.electacta.2022.140700) added measured
temperature and concentration dependent transport and thermal parameters for
the same cell family. The [authors’ parameter data](https://zenodo.org/records/5171874)
are public initial stock. This study assesses a particular decision use; it does
not claim to discover temperature dependence or establish a new charging method.

The [manufacturer-authored tentative 2016 specification](https://www.lithiumelements.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/m50.pdf)
supplies absolute current tiers, voltage regulation and termination. The common
charging envelope is 2.5–4.2 V and 0–45°C. Use 1.455 A at or below 25°C and
3.395 A only above 25°C, with CV at 4.2 V and a 50 mA termination. These are
source-supported operating bounds, not new physical qualification. O’Regan’s
installed 4.4 V upper/100%-SOC defaults are explicitly overridden in copied
parameter instances; source bytes remain intact.

The selected native census is three singleton operations: 36 backend forecasts
for the supplier and 12 for each recipient, totaling 60 attempted forecasts.
Every recipient enters all three recipes and four model views, including the
Chen attribution contrast, while freezing its selected recipe from the supplied
model before reference outcomes. Repeated uses of an identical cached trace add
no native contact. Three operation receipts and 60 backend forecasts are
different censuses; neither is a count of physical independent units.

The 60 forecasts count selected main charging simulations. Their canonical
`backend_solve_scope` is `MAIN_CHARGING_SUBSOLVES_ONLY`. Every actual
`solver.step` event-segment call is retained in the ordered `charging_attempts`
ledger; `backend_solve_calls` equals its length and can exceed one per forecast.
Each charging call requests IDAKLU internal consistent initialization. That
request is retained explicitly; it does not claim to observe internal Newton
iterations or count them as separate solver calls. Root, left/right receivers,
full requested and realized differential states and separate state digests,
control/input/model signatures, all native adaptive clocks, returns/errors and
measured CPU/wall remain in custody. Native initial-SOC preparation
also calls initial-state/SOH algebraic solvers. Each actual subsolver attempt,
including native composite/full-to-split fallbacks, retains its solver
configuration, processed model-expression digest, returned/error status and
measured CPU/wall expense in a separate initializer ledger. The composite
dispatcher adds no duplicate numerical solve. Its observed attempt count is not
silently called 60. Initializer expense windows are already contained in each
trace's complete expense and are not summed twice. The original SOH initializer and numerical tolerances remain the same.
The prospective controller and charging-call census change explicitly, while
the selected logical forecast roster remains 36+12+12.

Actual per-trace CPU/wall expense is allocated explicitly from the shared
forecast ledger. EL and the matched conventional full procedure receive the
same 36+12+12 traces; direct DFN receives its 18+6+6 primary/refined traces.
These are shared computations, not independent trials. Both complete and failed
attempts are charged. Complete-total-cost superiority remains unevaluable when
engineering or researcher assistance expense is unknown, even if a numerical
runtime comparison can be computed.

The native method follows the [official O’Regan PyBaMM mesh example](https://docs.pybamm.org/en/v25.8.0/source/examples/notebooks/models/simulating-ORegan-2022-parameter-set.html)
with right-exponential particle meshes; actual installed 26.6.2.0 source hashes
control this execution. Primary meshes are 30 electrode/40 particle points,
refined meshes 60/80, with IDAKLU tolerances 1e-7/1e-8 and 1e-9/1e-10. The
refined model is a nested numerical view, not another physical replicate.
The backend step-count test is disabled with `max_num_steps=-1`; its maximum
step size is unrestricted (`dt_max=0`) and no progress-count stop is introduced.
These values use [SUNDIALS' documented unlimited settings](https://sundials.readthedocs.io/en/latest/ida/Usage/index.html).
Native nonlinear/error-test convergence checks remain numerical validity
conditions and any technical failure is retained.

The prospective native controller uses smooth fixed-tier algebraic CC/CV
segments and actual native temperature roots. Within each segment its residual
is `min((4.2 V - V)/(1 V), (I + active magnitude)/(1 A)) = 0`.
LOW uses 1.455 A; HIGH uses 3.395 A. Primary views switch upward at
25°C + 0.001 K and downward at 25°C + 0.0005 K; the refined DFN uses
0.0001 K and 0.00005 K. These conservative numerical bands derive from the
frozen 0.01 K assessment precision divided by 10 and 100. They are an explicit
approximation to the ideal guard, not revised manufacturer limits or a claim
of exact-controller delivery. Every retained HIGH receiver must be strictly
above 25°C; an exactly 25°C receiver always has LOW eligibility. LOW can remain
conservative inside its declared band. The independent evaluator checks the
actual segment residual and the unchanged source envelope separately.

Pinned PyBaMM automatically discovers time-based Heaviside discontinuities;
it skips state comparisons such as the former temperature guard. IDAKLU's
root function includes termination events, while `SWITCH` events apply to
CasADi's fast-with-events path and `DISCONTINUITY` means a time known before
integration. The new method therefore uses a native state-root termination
followed by supported full-state continuation. The same built model carries
all actual scaled differential states, global clock, capacity and chamber;
only its algebraic state is reinitialized. Requested and realized states are
retained separately. Component differences must stay within the original
`atol + rtol * max(abs(left), abs(right))` numerical weights and capacity
continuity within 0.0001 Ah. A changed digest does not automatically imply
failed continuity, and a copied requested digest does not prove realization.

Both directional thresholds lie strictly above 25°C, avoiding a zero-root
restart and preventing HIGH continuation at an exact 25°C state. The native
root/roundoff residual allowance is epsilon/1000000 K; this is computational
root integrity, not physical accuracy. A genuinely nonadvancing returned
native model-time endpoint closes the forecast as a technical numerical
failure. There is no segment, probe, retry, CPU, memory or elapsed-time ceiling.
All failed subsolves and attempted continuations remain metered and retained.

Temperature displacement alone cannot bound transition time or charge bias.
The independent primary/refined DFN assessment jointly changes mesh,
localization band and the already frozen solver tolerances. Its 1 s, 1 mV,
0.01 K and current-integral/capacity checks determine conditional admission;
unresolved sensitivity stays unevaluable. This supplies no absolute
localization-accuracy or continuous-time certificate.

The ordinary 4.2 V terminating event is replaced by a 4.201 V numerical
protective event so CV can operate. Its nominal target remains 4.2 V and the
numerical assessment allowance remains 1 mV. Every target and protective event
is retained in every segment. The model-clock window remains 180000 seconds,
derived as `2.5 Ah / 0.05 A`; it is a consequence of the selected recipe,
not a CPU or wall-time ceiling.

Coordinated thermal/charging control and charge-to-80% endpoints are established
methods, including [integrated thermal control](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.04358v1)
and [joint electrical/thermal MPC](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.24889v1). Their
cell-specific settings and degradation claims are not transplanted here. The
current study compares conditional model adequacy and cumulative inquiry cost
under a declared finite menu; it does not claim global optimal control or
real-cell charging safety.
