## Scientific question

What must a measurement preserve, and can one operation supply a condition needed by another?

## Findings and unresolved questions

The analytical RC study identifies information lost when distinct futures
share the same permitted observation. Later numerical RC work separately tests
measurement, preparation supply and successful joined use.

The prerequisite chain reused eleven exposed preparations across three designs.
The original design achieved ten joined passes and one refusal. An expanded menu
supplied the missing preparation but missed the deadline; a separately extended
deadline permitted eleven joined passes. These are numerical development
findings, with no physical-board qualification or 33 independent confirmations.
The [RC results](records/recursive-prerequisite-results.md) retain the complete comparison.
