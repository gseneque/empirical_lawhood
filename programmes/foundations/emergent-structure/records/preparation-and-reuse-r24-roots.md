[Service, prediction loss and handoff errors](preparation-and-reuse-results.md#r24--can-a-forecast-supply-the-handoff-to-a-fixed-response-law)

The two choices in a cell belong to consumers 0 and 1, respectively. The last column gives normalized response loss for composed, cached and direct reuse, in that order. For the 8-unit pulse, choices 0 and 1 are negative and positive on port 0 and choices 2 and 3 are negative and positive on port 1. Choices 4 through 7 use the same order at magnitude 16. A false admission means that the receiver admitted the request and failed to deliver it.
