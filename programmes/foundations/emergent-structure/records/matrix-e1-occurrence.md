# E1 occurrence and first-admission persistence

The current compact E1 protocol estimates probabilities in four separately
assigned preparation populations. Each root is one independent unit. X/Y
sectors, PRIMARY/FINE views, time samples and later episodes are nested within
that root. The source, numerical assessment and retained missing-contact
census must cover every assigned root before reduction.

## Events and origins

The selected common lattice begins at zero. A factor satisfying the raw
associative, Lie and fit-angle admission rules at zero is initially present and
has admission time zero, without invented prehistory. Later admissions require
the selected trailing persistence and adjacent projector continuity. Frame
orientation ambiguity does not itself remove an identified factor. The origin
projector is fixed at the earliest observed qualifying admission.

The programme observes through tick 1152 and asks whether any qualifying
admission occurred by tick 1024. The first such admission supplies a 128-tick
follow-up. It never selects the longest or a later surviving episode. If an
earlier sample is unobserved or unidentifiable, a later observed admission
establishes occurrence but does not establish the first origin. Its
first-origin survival remains unknown.

Moving survival requires admission and adjacent projector continuity at every
follow-up check. Origin-fixed survival additionally requires the selected angle
to the original projector at every check. Joint survival requires both. Any
observed failure within the follow-up establishes failure even if another check
is unknown. Unknown samples alone establish neither survival nor exit. A root
with an unusable native or factor numerical assessment contributes unknown
scientific outcomes, while retaining its measured diagnostics and provenance.

Each root event separately records initial presence, occurrence, first observed
and first established admission, first admission after known absence, return
after a confirmed exit, identification-loss ticks, unobserved ticks and observed
exit ticks. Whole-root occupancy rows keep every assigned root at every lattice
time. Their first-causal columns describe observed admissions; exact first-origin
knowledge is in the event records. The protected factor products retain complete
raw observations, scalar-detector comparisons and both lifetime summaries for
further descriptive readouts.

## Probability intervals and missingness

The eight primary cells are occurrence and joint survival in PRIMARY-Y, one
pair for each family. E1 receives exact alpha 1/160. Bonferroni allocation gives
two-sided noncoverage 1/1280 per primary cell, or tail probability 1/2560.
X, FINE and separate moving/origin-fixed endpoints receive descriptive marginal
95% intervals, without an additional simultaneous claim. The original root
identities and categorical state sets are retained in every cell.

For known risk count n, s known successes and u unknown outcomes, the envelope
is `[CP_lower(s,n), CP_upper(s+u,n)]`. When eligibility is uncertain, take the
union over all allowed eligibility/outcome completions. The reducer computes
the extremal success counts for each feasible risk count and then the extrema
of their exact Clopper–Pearson bounds. If no eligible root is established,
conditional bounds remain undefined even when some roots might be eligible.
Complete-case intervals are separately labelled by their assessable-only
counts; they do not replace the assigned-population envelope.

There is no minimum-80 risk gate, probability-above-half threshold or automatic
binary support claim. A scientifically usable characterisation has status
`PARTIAL` and reason `e1-estimation-map-no-binary-claim`; a census with no usable
observations has status `UNEVALUABLE`. Each cell still reports its own risk and
missingness. Wide intervals, zero risk and adverse observations are retained.

## Reproduction and scope

The [public input assembly](../workflows/matrix-programme/e1-inputs.md)
binds these choices before D and C. Current criterion, source, trace, factor
product and population-result records have distinct version 2 identities.
Historical products and their exact bytes are not converted into fresh evidence.
The compact retained reader authenticates original publications, independently
checks native arrays and factor witnesses without acquisition or eigensolves,
then recomputes root events, intervals and the final adjudication.

A full read with current reveal authority also supports an authenticated
`FAILED` or `BLOCKED` operational terminal. It independently replays complete
assessed roots and retains every other assigned root as unavailable or unknown,
with its original task terminal, recovery events and any durable native contact.
The same interval reducer produces a distinct `CLOSED_CUSTODY_REDUCTION`; its
adjudication is absent. Completed but incompletely assessed phase outputs retain
custody without becoming an assessed root. No completion receipt is invented.

An explicit partial read remains deferred. An absent operational terminal,
missing required recovery event, corrupt publication or invalid current reveal
refuses scientific reconstruction. A terminal-only retryable block retains that
original observation without inventing an event. Reading a closed run grants no
native reentry, sampling warrant or scientific qualification.

The map characterises the declared finite preparation populations. Initial
presence is not spontaneous emergence, later recurrence is not another
independent observation, and observed persistence does not establish an
autonomous subsystem or a thermodynamic phase.
