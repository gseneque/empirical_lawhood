At each observed handoff take two sibling measurements from that checkpoint. M1 measures structural eligibility and the H64 FACTOR impulse on two signed/HOLD panels. Delivery requires preservation on every measured branch and signed impulse of at least 0.10 actuator units on each panel. Retain a delivery floor of 0.75, wrong-offer ceiling of 0.10 per assignment, allowable paired coverage loss of 1/16, and the original force-call and simultaneous-uncertainty rules. Show wrong offers per offer as well as per assignment. Charge each retained physical acquisition once and report each policy's stand-alone cost.

M7 switches to the declared terminal couplings, meters the switch work and applies zero probe force. Record the initial occupancy and time to the common interior within 1,024 reference ticks. The interior is within 0.025 of the ideal coordinate in either direction. At each entry seal the sign of the displacement and the sign of the incoming one-reference-tick increment. Record missing prior samples explicitly. Launch four coupled futures from the same entry to 4.096 model time, using commitment boundaries at ±0.1 and a 16-reference-tick dwell. Numerical refinements share the observed physical entry and use equivalent dwell times. Report target, competing exit, censoring, numerical failure, structural nonentry, clock nonentry and failure to reach the physical interior separately.

The recursive result's `paired_policy_handoffs` table links M7's source to the M1 acquisition for the same root. Each slot records the checkpoint digest, preceding sample, actual physical time, trailing observation window and wait to entry or the search deadline. A missing acquisition keeps the root in the table. A shared tick with a different state fails the check.

For the policy-clock design, each M7 entry summary reports how often each sealed cue called the target, called the competing exit or was unavailable. Its constant competing-exit reference uses every observed entrant and retains the original missing-future bounds. The entry wait starts at the assigned policy's actual handoff. Estimate target-before-competing-exit service and entry probability per assigned root. Conditional incidence and restricted mean time use the actual entrants. For a paired conditional comparison, include roots entering under both policies and identify that denominator. Four futures from one entry contribute one independent root. The recorded sign forecasts are graded on the same entrants and ages, retaining unknown-cue and unknown-outcome bounds.

With the original M1 module allowance of 0.01, 128 complete roots give a simultaneous paired half-width of 0.33953. The coverage comparison alone then requires at least 36 net additional deliveries. At least 111 deliveries, at most three wrong assignments and at least 82 cheaper roots are also required, with a positive mean force-call saving. Under the illustrative original T=2 rates of one gain per 16 roots and zero harms, the exact probability of reaching 36 net gains is approximately 1.18 × 10⁻¹⁴. This is a design sensitivity calculation. The measured effects and uncertainty remain reportable if the complete policy fails to qualify.
