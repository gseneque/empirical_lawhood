## R23 · Does a fixed preparation restore applicability?

This experiment holds the original 24-coordinate response law F, its coefficients and uncertainty fixed. Three schedules share one noisy six-matrix prefix and one bath waveform, D016. They start at tick 4096. H continues the original evolution, N applies the nominated negative Y excursion and P applies the positive schedule. At tick 4496 the receiver observes the resulting states and commits its choices. Two future draws and two numerical views measure the signed responses. Each root carries 256 paired requests. The root is the independent unit. [Design, eligibility and decision rules](empirical-lawhood.constructed-preparation-applicability.md)

**Constructor qualification.** Seven of eight independent roots crossed the force-rate support face with the required margin in both numerical views. Six were required. All eight N roots passed all seven validity checks. The H and P schedules each passed the six other checks and failed support on every root. N delivered both tasks with full validity on 1,993 of 2,048 request pairs, with zero false admissions. H and P delivered zero covered pairs. These eight roots qualified the constructor for the independent evaluation.

The initial eight-root attempt acquired its eight prefixes, then its selection worker failed before any handoff or assay. It used 98,304 native intervals. The replacement used eight distinct roots and 375,936 intervals across all 50 tasks. Its report SHA-256 is `efff34f0a8954f035c2246be887fa94c740748f4030aada070bc0c77041d54e5`.
