## Purpose and interpretation of SCI02

The target is cumulative predictive capability with less new acquisition per supported use.
A suitable description preserves the distinctions required by its downstream receiver.
It also retains uncertainty, information cutoffs, physical scope and acquisition cost.
A missing observation or preparation becomes a further scientific requirement.
An assessed supplier can then supply that requirement for later use.

The intended Rubisco connection is:

`available history/preparation -> reusable response description -> paired physical contribution -> joint kinetic contrasts -> WT/I95M forecast`.

Each arrow requires its own supply and consumer-admission evidence.
RUB02's four effective contrasts reduce the required endpoint description.
They do not supply the missing physical evidence.
E4 and SCI01 inform the next description through operator change, driven directions and signed error.
Their unavailable fitted relation and exposed analyses supply no molecular error bound.

SCI02 DEV8 supports preparation-dependent usefulness.
EARLY lowered error on every root for every method.
Its updated Krylov error reduction relative to ORIGINAL was approximately 44–61%.
Frozen FULL and Krylov passed only one EARLY root each.
Updated FULL passed all eight.
The permitted new observation therefore remained consequential.

Updated Krylov met error and signal criteria on all eight EARLY roots.
Its numerical-view criterion passed five.
The point selector falsely admitted one of six reduced admissions.
Both selectors always chose EARLY, with no observed recipe-choice gain over fixed EARLY.
This result distinguishes preparation benefit, predictive support and selection value.

Every retained recipe contrast improved under refresh in all eight roots for FULL and Krylov.
Updated Krylov retained greater contrast error than updated FULL in every comparison.
These nested comparisons retain eight independent roots.
They identify a paired-description problem without reversing SCI01's findings for different contrasts.

Three shadows cost approximately 4.013 CPU seconds per root.

Fixed EARLY updated-Krylov computation components averaged approximately 0.682 seconds, against 1.885 seconds for EARLY native computation.
These component timings identify an opportunity and an expensive selector.
They establish neither a complete policy saving nor a molecular speedup.

The unchanged SCI02 selector remains closed.
