This guide derives selected E5 and E6 programmes from original evidence.
It preserves each selected account's scientific and authority boundaries.
Select the account, phase and authentic original inputs before derivation.

The protocols are `matrix.selected-prepared.declarative-development` and `matrix.selected-prepared.declarative-protected`.
They require no precomputed programme, graph, task map or resource-cell roster.
Derivation, authoring and acquisition require the commitment-bearing input
contracts below.

Protected `experiment-read` authenticates the original issued run, current
execution/reveal grants, complete receipt closure and exact terminal scientific
reduction for either selected account. It retains source qualification separately
from downstream calibration and admission. A separate native-assay diagnostic
retains measured functional errors even when a point forecast is unavailable;
it does not change the original terminal reduction or supply an admission bound.
Explicit partial reads of the matched revision retain an unpublished partial
assessment under current execution/reveal grants, including an admission whose
future is missing. Absent targets remain unknown and keep their assigned
denominator. Closed point-only accounts defer scientific products on partial reads.
