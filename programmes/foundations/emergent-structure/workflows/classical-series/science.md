substrate. It revisits the two capstone connections, E1–E4, the retained causal
refresh and signed-contrast analyses, and the questions in the unentered E5/E6
plans through one recursive implementation. It does not overwrite the original
protocols, original evidence or historical decisions. Its preparation population,
common receiver, task menu and inference are declared here and in its scientific
settings. A new development execution does not become a fresh protected

The scientific implementation is
`src/empirical_lawhood/domains/matrix/classical_series/science.py`.
It exposes `normalise_config`, `acquire_native_root`, `summarise_native_root`,
`calibrate_native_roots` and `analyse_native_root`. The framework owns source

momenta, arranged as two triplets. The complete state therefore has 96 real
position coordinates and 96 real momentum coordinates, including every trace
component. The native potential, real Hilbert–Schmidt coordinates, unit kinetic
mass and complex128 arithmetic are those of the existing six-matrix domain.
The implementation calls its existing `MatrixParameterCoordinate`,
`advance_parameter_pair`, `scheduled_force_step` and `hessian_image` owners.

## Independent units and shared random inputs

One assigned root is one independent unit. Coordinates, preparation arms,
actions, signs, future panels, numerical views, receivers and time samples are
nested within that root. Neither a 48-component receiver nor an endpoint pair
creates additional independent roots.

A root's PCG64DXSM streams use a fixed `SeedSequence` with separate purpose
identifiers for preparation, preparation bridge, future panel, future bridge and
physical force chart. All coordinates and preparation arms share their root's
standardized innovations. Each coordinate uses its own temperature and friction
when converting those innovations into physical Langevin noise. Primary and fine
views use the existing exact weighted Ornstein–Uhlenbeck bridge. The resulting
nonlinear trajectories need not coincide.

TRAIN, CAL and CONF rosters must be disjoint. The calibration supplier consumes
compact, authenticated summaries of TRAIN/CAL acquisitions. It receives no CONF
result. CONF acquisition consumes the frozen supplier before beginning its
native work. Root forecasts are calculated and hashed before the corresponding
assay future is drawn or advanced. A reanalysis consumes the original acquisition
and original frozen supplier; it cannot refit the model while calling the result
a read of the earlier experiment.

Distinct pseudorandom roots and causal implementation ordering are software
facts. The scientific sampling assumptions needed for population statements
remain explicit premises. The exposed development route does not provide a
protected external reveal service.

## Common physical task and method menu

The receiver is a fixed physical chart generated by the reference right Pauli
factor and the identity `I4/2`, independently of a realized trajectory. There
are 24 position and 24 momentum outputs. The same chart is used at every
parameter endpoint and preparation arm. This is an intentional adaptation of
historical experiments in which an admitted origin factor could supply a
state-dependent chart. It makes the new high-minus-low and prepared-minus-baseline
comparisons refer to the same physical output.

The FACTOR, GENERIC, COMPLEMENT and TRACE force directions have unit real-HS
norm. The fixed reference factor defines the force role, while the actual
observed Y factor supplies the FACTOR *reduction* when its identification and
raw algebra-admission criteria are met. These are separate operations. The
complete plus, minus and zero-force HOLD branches share each panel's future
innovations. No failed reduction removes its native comparison from the roster.

The response is half the positive-minus-negative physical state difference,
projected by the common receiver. It is not divided again by force amplitude.
At elapsed time `t` and `tau = min(t, pulse_duration)`, scoring uses the original
actuator scales

`position_scale = amplitude * tau * (t - tau/2)` and
`momentum_scale = amplitude * tau`.

A block error is the Euclidean norm of the 24 scaled components. The separate
`curve_rms` statistic is explicitly a component/time RMS and does not replace the
block acceptance metric. All nested panels and views remain in the maximum
error used by the declared root admission rule.

| Method | Representation and meaning |

| FULL | The complete 96-position Hessian and 192-state linearized BAOAB map |

Their comparison is not described as an equal-rank performance win. Actual ranks,

are not silently interchanged.

## Causal repair and signed contrasts

The frozen model uses only the origin Hessian. Its causal repair keeps the
origin representation and obtains the physical Hessian of the independent
zero-force HOLD state at the declared response checkpoint. The subsequent
reduced propagation starts from the original reduced checkpoint state; it is
not reset to a later native response or refitted to the assay outcome.

For each available high/low cross-coupling pair at the same temperature,
friction and preparation arm, the readout retains signed errors in both native
views, every future panel, action and receiver block. It calculates

`S = ||e_high||² + ||e_low||²`,
`A = 2 <e_high,e_low>`, and `D = S - A = ||e_high-e_low||²`.

Both-endpoint improvement and contrast deterioration are separate booleans.
The historical 197/240 reversal count is not an acceptance target for a new
population, physical chart or source version.

The reused generic propagation accounting computes inherited and generated
suffix discrepancies from the actual full map, actual reduced map and lift.
It preserves the distinction between projecting the Hessian before composing a
step and projecting an already composed full step. The sum is checked against
direct propagation. Native prediction error and full-minus-reduced representation
error are reported as different quantities.

## Use of both mathematical developments

The derivation stage uses an actual native physical Hessian as the Hermitian
operator, and joint force/receiver ports as its seeds. It reports acquired prefix
rank and operator actions, the residual enclosure at a nonreal spectral point,
and comparison with the full 96-dimensional solve. A finite acquired complement
supplies an exact Schur-tail control. A full finite Hessian change is represented
and checked by the exact joint resolvent update. Full rank is reported honestly;
local molecular chemistry is not assumed to imply a low-rank operator update.

The BAOAB phase transition is not treated as self-adjoint. An indefinite native
Hessian remains indefinite. The static Gaussian compliance calculation is
performed only for a positive definite snapshot; otherwise it is explicitly not
applicable. There is no eigenvalue clipping or undeclared positive shift.
Even a positive snapshot does not establish a globally quadratic native
potential, a canonical native ensemble or a bounded nonlinear remainder.

The finite nonlinear elimination controls include the stated two-dimensional
quartic example with unchanged origin Hessians and nonzero paired free-energy
change. Orders 64 and 96 test quadrature refinement. A separate four-dimensional
quadratic environment with finite retained nonlinearity exercises actual hidden
coordinate elimination and its Schur compliance. Two harmonic environments with
the same static Schur complement but `cos(t)` and `cos(2t)` memory demonstrate the
static/dynamic distinction. An analytic restrained Gaussian pair exercises the
finite-grid anchor-plus-variance identity with signed, nonuniform weights and a
single explicit conversion from kJ/mol to J/mol.

These controls establish implementation agreement in their stated classes.
They do not turn a driven matrix history into an equilibrium molecular measure.
A small sampled nonlinear residual is not a uniform remainder bound. A frequency
residual enclosure can be correct yet too wide to justify any native work being
skipped.
