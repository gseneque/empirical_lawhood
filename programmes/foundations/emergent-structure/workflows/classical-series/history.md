**Authored consolidation date: 16 November 2026, Australia/Sydney.**

This document maps the complete scientific sequence to the consolidated v1.0
investigation. The new investigation is an explicitly authored successor on the
same classical substrate. It does not replace original studies, change their
decisions, or claim that a small software control reproduces a population result.
The [execution guide](../../../../../docs/v1.0/classical-matrix-series.md) explains
the editable definition, derived graph, original-result access and continuation.

## 1. What is carried forward

The substrate is six Hermitian 4-by-4 matrices, with 96 real position and 96
momentum coordinates. The trace coordinates remain present. The system is
classical, stochastic and driven; Hermitian coordinates do not make this a
quantum trajectory experiment. The native potential, physical BAOAB map,
scheduled couplings and Hermitian noise implementation retain their current
scientific owners. The potential coefficients called `mass_x` and `mass_y`
are not kinetic masses. Potential cross coupling and bath friction are distinct.

The original [E1–E6 contract](../../records/matrix-programme.md) supplies the
physical and statistical definitions. Its actual records retain authority over
their original experiments. New choices in the consolidated definition have new
implementation and design identities. Disjoint pseudo-random streams establish
computational separation; a population-independence or exchangeability claim
still requires its scientific assumptions.

There are three different uses of earlier work:

| Use | Appropriate evidence | What it establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Numerical migration control | Identical exposed physical operands run through the old and new native implementations | Preservation on the tested corpus; no population result |
| Historical reconstruction | Authenticated original designs, arrays, receipts, masks and selected read authority | A readout of those exact earlier observations, retaining their exposure and missingness |
| New authored experiment | A new frozen definition, causal dependency graph and disjoint assigned root banks | New findings under that successor's explicit population and procedures |

The supplied branch archive contains source and documentary summaries. The
complete historical matrix raw custody is external. Its absence cannot be
repaired by regenerating plausible arrays or assigning old identities to a new
run. The old [report availability record](../../records/release-results.md)
identifies what the public deposit actually includes.

## 3. Original decisions remain intact

The original E1–E6 allocation allowed at most 980 independent groups and 1,636
native cases, with distinct original phase assignments. It allocated eight
primary slots at alpha 1/160; E1 used simultaneous interval allocations, E5 a
paired discordance criterion, and E3/E4/E6 their declared win criteria. Those
thresholds cannot be transplanted into a successor with different targets,
sample sizes, families, availability or analysis rules.

The original E4 failure produced twelve conditional nonentries across later
calibration, confirmation, preparation and transfer. No E5 benefit or E6 transfer
was measured. The new design reopens the *question* with new suppliers and a new
identity. It does not rewrite the old nonentry as a successful experiment.

## 4. What the two derivations add

The first derivation concerns finite changes in retained nonlinear coordinates
coupled to a quadratic environment. Its reusable implementation is
`empirical_lawhood.methods.retained_gaussian_response`. It already supplies
conditional Gaussian elimination, exact finite retained modifications,
normalization, restrained partition/moment calculations, residual-error
propagation, compliance bounds and harmonic memory with preparation terms.
The [method guide](../../../../../docs/development/retained-gaussian-response.md)
states its scope and independent numerical controls.

The second derivation supplies joint input/receiver geometry. It preserves
cross signs, phases and overlaps, supports finite operator changes, and connects
finite Krylov information to a response through residual and polynomial bounds.
Its reusable owner is `empirical_lawhood.methods.joint_response`. The supplied
manuscript additionally motivates signed endpoint-error accounting and the
exact inherited/generated representation decomposition after a causal update.

The following distinctions govern their use in the native experiment:

| Object or assertion | Required interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Native fixed-state Hessian | A self-adjoint stiffness in the declared real Hilbert–Schmidt chart. It may be indefinite. |
| BAOAB phase-state map | An ordered discrete dynamical map; it is generally not self-adjoint. Use its actual composed map for suffix accounting. |
| Positive reference operator | A separately justified assumption for static compliance/Gaussian equilibrium. A shifted or eigenvalue-clipped Hessian is a different declared model. |
| Dense eliminated complement | An exact finite oracle which charges for the complement. Its exactness does not demonstrate reduced acquisition. |
| Approximate or periodic tail | A selected continuation model with a residual/error account. A converged fixed-point residual alone is not a physical tail certificate. |
| Sampled nonlinear residual | Evidence at the evaluated states; it is not a uniform bound over the integration domain. |
| Centred response spectrum | Fluctuation or response information. It supplies neither an absolute canonical mean anchor nor an entire restraint-centre family. |
| Finite-grid thermodynamic target | The declared quadrature functional with its sign, weights, stiffness, temperature and units. It is distinct from a continuous endpoint free energy and from an activation barrier. |
| Finite-record variance | A weighted joint covariance calculation retaining cross-arm and temporal terms. It does not prove stationarity, mixing or equilibrium. |

The new matrix experiments can establish where a supplier is useful on this
known substrate. They cannot establish a molecular applicability assumption by
analogy. That assumption must be supplied and tested at the molecular receiver.

## 5. Iteration without rewriting evidence

The original research progressed through predeclared studies, exposed failures,
mechanism analysis and successor designs. The consolidated workflow represents
those as different operations and scientific scopes. Native acquisition retains
the physical operands once. Readout authenticates retained results. A new
analysis is an explicitly identified consumer of existing operands; it does not
reacquire trajectories or inherit prospective status. A new confirmation bank
is a separately authored successor with frozen choices and its own assigned
denominator.

Changing a hypothesis, numerical method, receiver, cutoff, preparation or
calibration changes the relevant identity. It does not justify mutating an
old result directory. Keep the earlier attempt and its negative/unavailable
outcomes, then use a new output directory and recorded ancestry. A failed
operational step and a falsified scientific comparison remain separate.

## 6. Rubisco-facing decisions

This programme is useful to Rubisco when its result changes a concrete supplier
decision. The relevant questions are whether a paired receiver needs omitted
cross information, whether a preparation makes a shorter description adequate,
whether causal refresh preserves a mutation contrast, whether memory improves
the needed finite horizon, and whether a mean anchor plus a centre-dependent
variance family can answer the actual finite restraint functional.

An exact identity or smaller basis is not a saving. Report the complete finite
workload: preparation, operator actions, training, calibration, native references,
failed work, certification, analysis, custody, fallback and future reuse count.
Actual avoided acquisitions, measured component timings and unexecuted cost
counterfactuals have separate fields. If all references were acquired for the
study, actual avoided reference work is zero even when a retrospective policy
would have selected fewer references.

The retained matrix results do not establish WT/I95M molecular construction,
equilibrium-state coverage, a rate, carboxylation/oxygenation competition or
specificity. Their value is a more precise and cheaper *candidate supplier* for
one of those still explicit physical requirements, or a well-supported reason
to reject that supplier.
