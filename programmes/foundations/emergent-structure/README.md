## Scientific question

When does an organised structure support useful response prediction, and can preparation extend that use?

## Findings and unresolved questions

The matrix investigations distinguish geometric organisation, dynamical
commitment and useful response prediction. The preprint's forecast and preparation
studies tested two separate enabling connections. Their independent units and
operating conditions remain distinct.

The retained release programme reached E4 development and revision. The selected
E4 relation was unavailable, so dependent calibration, confirmation, preparation
and transfer phases retained conditional nonentry. Scientific qualification
remains NOT_ESTABLISHED. The [release findings](records/release-results.md)
identify the completed report scope and its availability.
