**Martin-Avila 2020** supplies the strongest new assessment opportunity: a
homogeneous potato LSU/SSU panel at 25 °C, with native pS1 means **3.0 s⁻¹ /
84.5**, pS2 **3.0 / 82.6**, pS3 **3.5 / 87.3**, and pST **4.4 / 70.6**. It
contains both a favourable joint point contrast and a specificity sacrifice.
Turnover counts 5–6 and specificity counts 10–11 retain separate roles; they do
not establish paired independent preparations. Substitution directions differ
between abstract and narrative, so exact protein recipes must come from native
sequence/construct evidence before modelling. The public Dryad deposition
contains alignment/tree assets, without raw kinetic measurements. This is a
green-plant external assessment opportunity, not high-specificity red-parent
transport support. [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7474299/).

**Spreitzer 2005** supplies direct turnover and specificity for WT, LSU penta,
SSU ABSO and combined views in one series: **2.32/60**, **1.04/61**,
**1.19/61**, and **1.28/67**. Simple log addition predicts about **0.5334/62.0167**
for the combined point. This is a useful background-dependence challenge, but
resident SSU-family and single-rbcS assemblies require explicit joins before
calling it an exact homogeneous factorial. The additional triple row and
separate Table 3 series remain in their native roles. [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1287997/).

**Genkov 2009** provides an SSU alanine-substitution panel and an instability
exclusion. Six variants have joint kinetic constants and near-parent specificity
despite large carboxylation-activity losses. Native maximal carboxylation is
**mass-normalized activity**, rather than independently active-site-normalized
turnover. Retain that quantity role when using the panel as an adverse
mechanistic test. [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2781565/).

**Zhou 2014** supplies SSU E49V/D82G turnover **6.7 ± 0.6 → 14.6 ± 1.1 s⁻¹**,
with five to six reported independent experiments. Its native Table 1 lacks
specificity and oxygenation endpoints; affinity/efficiency gains cannot fill
that missing role. It is a genuine turnover lead with unknown joint benefit,
without all four single/double-mutant corners. [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4085280/).

## Reusable physical code and its missing response map

**Amritkar 2025** provides WT G. partita **1BWV** paired-subunit MD and separate
CO₂/O₂ contacts, with CABP, Mg²⁺ and carbamylated Lys retained. The MIT-licensed
author repository includes structures, simulation systems, analysis and
streamlined extracted values. The trajectory deposition totals approximately
26.1 GB; its system census extends beyond the primary study. Gas contacts use
a 6 Å threshold and separately simulated high-concentration gas reservoirs;
subunit dispersion cannot become independent biological n. These are reusable
WT mechanistic features, without productive chemistry, I95M effects or a
validated joint-kinetic response map. Prefer the small extracted values to bulk
trajectory acquisition if a named assessment requires them.
[Primary paper](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/42/1/msae268/7934943),
[author code](https://github.com/kacarlab/RuBisCO_evolution),
[simulation archive](https://zenodo.org/records/14187581).

Published reaction calculations offer ingredients with narrower scopes.
Douglas-Gallardo's carboxylation calculation uses a spinach LSU dimer, omitting
the SSU-only perturbation. Okude's fragment-rate work omits SSU, oxygenation
and enolization and substantially underpredicts turnover. Neither is an
available I95M two-endpoint simulator. An assessed reduced response relation
could suffice; a complete microscopic cycle is not imposed by default.
[Douglas-Gallardo primary supporting collection](https://acs.figshare.com/collections/Carbon_Dioxide_Fixation_in_RuBisCO_Is_Protonation-State-Dependent_and_Irreversible/6102511),
[Okude primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9453937/).

Recent computational precedents strengthen the conventional comparator.
Young's released active-learning code provides iterative acquisition/ranking,
while the actual inspected archive lacks its advertised numerical embedding
arrays. RubisGen supplies generative design and laboratory feedback, but its
non-Form-I designs and 24-hour product endpoint do not supply the intrinsic
paired-subunit response required here.
[Young code](https://github.com/jamesyoung93/Rubisco_Protein_Language_Model_Active_Learning),
[RubisGen primary preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.04.749267v1.full).
