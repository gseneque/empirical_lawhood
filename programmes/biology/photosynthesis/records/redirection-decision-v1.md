## Question, observable and requesting decision

Can a matched carbon-adduct processing model explain H287N's changed productive/
return competition under its own-WT assay, and does D263N show a different
computed response? The requesting intervention decision is whether downstream
carbon commitment is a credible target or hazard filter for active-site-network
edits. This is initially a source-domain mechanism decision. It is not a
beneficial-edit or G. partita SSU prediction.

The distinguishing observable is each own-background mutant/WT change in
`log(h/d)`, where h reaches a specified committed productive basin and d returns
by decarboxylation from the same chemically identified carbon adduct:

```text
log(h/d) = log(Ah/Ad) - (G‡forward - G‡return)/(RT)
Dg = log(h/d)mutant - log(h/d)own-WT
```

selected carbon-adduct proton/water lineages, giving four competitions and

The present enolate roster is not this adduct roster.

## Assessment and candidate limits

H287 CKABP hydrolysis/decarboxylation ratios are WT≥0.9 and N≤0.1. Under the
restricted two-exclusive-exits/no-recapture observation model and a common
applicable prefactor ratio, support requires at least a ninefold reduction in
local h/d: `DH287 ≤ -ln(9)`, or a competition-barrier shift≥5.4103 kJ/mol at
23°C. This is a conditional mechanism target, not measured qC or a fitted rate.
The paper permits isolated hydration deficiency and concerted turnover chemistry;
CKABP processing is not automatically normal gas-adduct commitment. Its
acid-stable numerator can include terminal misprocessing products such as
pyruvate; it is not automatically yield into committed productive PGA.
That product mapping is another explicit premise of the restricted criterion.

D263N has **no measured CKABP ratio**. Its 6.28→0.82 s^-1 turnover and 9.2→8.8
specificity do not label its carbon commitment as unchanged. Treat D263N as an
unlabelled mechanistic comparator, retaining finite-CO2 and activation controls.
Never pool its25°C/His6/CABP-normalized WT with the23°C H287 preparation.

Freeze chemically justified proton/water/QM-boundary/preparation alternatives,
barrier uncertainty and independently defensible prefactor/transmission ranges
before interpreting a result. No genotype-specific fitted prefactors, convenient

Assess retained complete path/basin operands independently of the path supplier.
