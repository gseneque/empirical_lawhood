## Challenge requirement and quantity definitions

The current challenge requires a candidate's specificity to reach that of **Galdieria partita** and its carboxylation turnover to reach that of **maize**, measured in paired enzyme assays with the respective controls. Its page does not specify a strain, cultivar, temperature, buffer, preparation, numeric threshold or uncertainty decision rule. Those omissions remain unresolved external acceptance conditions. The submission-first local objective does not require new wet-lab measurements during development. [Official challenge](https://millenniumproblems.bio/).

For the audited sources, intrinsic carboxylation turnover means carboxylation events per **Rubisco active site** per second at stated substrate saturation and activation. It is distinct from leaf assimilation, active-enzyme yield, initial activation status or activity per total protein mass. Relative specificity is the dimensionless ratio **Sᶜ/ᵒ = VᶜKᵒ/(VᵒKᶜ)**. Carboxylation efficiency **kcatᶜ/Kᶜ**, and the CO₂ affinity measured in ambient oxygen **Kᶜ(1 + O/Kᵒ)**, are different quantities. The latter cannot replace oxygen-free Kᶜ in the specificity expression without an explicit correction. [Uemura 1997 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9144578/), [Sharwood 2008 primary methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2230571/).

## Maize assay controls and method dependence

Sharwood 2016 uses native leaf protein from plants grown at 28/22 °C day/night, sampled 5–7 h into the light period and frozen in liquid nitrogen. Extraction is in N₂-sparged EPPS buffer, pH 8.0, with EDTA, DTT, protease inhibitor and PVPP. The study varies MgCl₂ and centrifugation duration when investigating activation artifacts; those experimental variations must not be flattened into one universal extraction condition. Its recommended rapid extraction includes approximately 5 mM MgCl₂.

The coupled Rubisco assay uses **25 °C, 100 mM EPPS-NaOH pH 8.0, 10 mM MgCl₂, 20 mM NaHCO₃, 0.4 mM RuBP**, NADH, ATP, phosphocreatine and coupling enzymes. Total activity follows **10–15 min preactivation without RuBP**; initial activity uses immediate addition to RuBP-containing buffer. Four NADH molecules are consumed per carboxylation event. The study identifies slower coupled-assay results across species, including purified tobacco controls, and describes the limitation as unresolved. Its full-activation evidence must not be converted into a universal activation time for another enzyme. [Native methods, Table 1 and assay discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4867899/).

The cited **Sharwood 2008** radiometric method uses **25 °C, pH 8.0**, 100 mM Hepps-NaOH, 15 mM MgCl₂, 0.6 mM RuBP and 0.1 mg ml⁻¹ carbonic anhydrase in septum-capped, CO₂-free-air-equilibrated vials. The Vcmax assay contains **15 mM NaH¹⁴CO₃**, stops after **1 min** with formic acid and measures acid-stable fixation by scintillation counting. The method activates extract with **15 mM each NaHCO₃/MgCl₂ for 10 min**, and estimates active sites from duplicate aliquots with **10 or 30 μM [¹⁴C]CABP** followed by gel filtration. These are **referenced-method details**, not individually verified buffer/sample records for each 2016 maize observation. Dissolved substrate CO₂ is not numerically equal to total bicarbonate. [Primary radiometric and active-site methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2230571/).

The 2016 Table 1 n is explicitly **leaf protein samples**, not technical repeats; the paper does not expose a complete plant/sample/preparation join for those table rows. It supplies no maize specificity measurement from the same material. The native source CSV includes other maize contexts: **Carella** turnover **4.1 ± 0.6** and specificity **87.3 ± 1.4** in separate rows with pH **8/8.2**, Kubien turnover **4.05 ± 0.04**, and primary and secondary older specificity views. These are separate observations with different conditions and error meanings, not a pooled reference distribution. The Kubien row's note distinguishes turnover pH 8, Km pH 8.1 and specificity method pH 8.3 despite a global pH 8 cell.

The CSV's `maize_uemura96` row names **Uemura 1996** but its PMID/citation instead identify the 1997 thermophilic-algae study. The authentic 1996 method abstract describes an **open gas system** and direct chromatographic separation of PGA and phosphoglycolate. It does not expose the maize cultivar or full assay conditions. Preserve its native **S 96.4 ± 1.7, pH 8.3, 25 °C** cells as a compilation view rather than assigning them to Kelvedon Glory. [Primary 1996 method abstract](https://academic.oup.com/pcp/article/37/3/325/1930998).

## Galdieria identity and preparation limits

The authentic AB018008 record explicitly states **“whether initiation codon GTG codes for Met or Val is unknown”** for rbcS. The annotated 138-residue translation begins **MRIT…**, whereas the deposited 1BWV sequence begins **VRIT…**, also at 138 residues. Thus a silent processing explanation, truncation or offset correction is unsupported. UniProt references the 1998 Tokara nucleotide submission and the 1999/2002 structures; it does not cite the 1997 kinetic preparation as a verified sequence measurement. The deposited LSU's carbamylated Lys is a chemical activation state, not an engineered substitution. Author residue numbering also differs from linear sequence numbering; use the retained explicit correspondence when specifying edits. [Native nucleotide record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/api/embl/AB018008.1?download=false), [native PDB entry](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1BWV).

Taxonomic and material views need separate keys. The old compilation also names **Cyanidium partita**, `primary=0`, with the same 1.6/6.6/238 values and an unknown-primary-source note. That row does not authenticate a second independent enzyme or prove a nomenclatural synonym. **G. sulfuraria** measurements and **G. partita** measurements cannot be substituted solely because the genus or high specificity is shared. Existing ENA and current UniProt records use different higher-rank taxonomy annotations while sharing species taxon **83374**; retain annotation versions rather than treating the changed lineage as a new biochemical identity.

The acquired **Shibata 1996** crystallization report purifies Galdieria using a referenced Porphyra procedure from Uemura 1996. It forms a quaternary complex with **20 mM MgCl₂, 20 mM NaHCO₃ and 2 mM CABP**, crystallized at **298 K**. These are crystallographic preparation conditions, not the specificity assay's pH, dissolved gases or turnover normalization. Its accessible text does not supply a Tokara culture voucher or a new joint kinetic table. [Primary crystallization report](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/biochemistry1922/120/6/120_6_1064/_pdf).

The 1999 structural abstract repeats specificity **238**. The 2002 primary structural introduction explicitly quotes **238 at 25 °C / 364 at 15 °C**, citing the earlier kinetic study. Neither quotation supplies a new independently measured control. The 1997 abstract attributes the strong discrimination to differing carboxylation/oxygenation activation energies, but does not expose an edit-response calibration. Its original full table and methods remain inaccessible in this bounded acquisition. Consequently **25 °C/pH 8, error 8.8 and turnover 1.6 remain catalogue/quotation fields**, with the exact original assay implementation unresolved. [1999 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10336462/), [2002 primary structural article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0014579302031484).

## What the submission packet can specify now

A defensible **proposed post-submission test** can retain the unchanged target through two reference ratios:

```text
turnover ratio = candidate kcatᶜ / simultaneously assayed maize kcatᶜ
specificity ratio = candidate Sᶜ/ᵒ / simultaneously assayed G. partita Sᶜ/ᵒ
joint target: both ratios ≥ 1, for the same frozen candidate LSU/SSU identity
```

These define the comparison without replacing either reference with a convenient compiled mean. They are a proposed operationalization, not an organizer-approved protocol. A packet can now name **Kelvedon Glory** as a traceable proposed maize control and **G. partita Tokara**, identified against **AB018008.1**, as the proposed Galdieria lineage/paired nucleotide reference. Neither should be represented as an already authenticated reference preparation. Unedited AB018008.1 specifies a possible recombinant reference recipe, with its native GTG initiation codon retained; equivalent assembly, mature processing and kinetics would require post-submission verification. A native preparation is a different material view and needs its own specimen/sequence identity. The genotype cannot silently authenticate either expression route.

The author can select shared **25 °C**, **pH 8.0**, purified preparations, a direct carboxylation readout and a direct competing-gas product readout as working protocol assumptions. A concrete turnover starting recipe is the referenced radiometric **100 mM Hepps-NaOH, 15 mM MgCl₂, 0.6 mM RuBP, 15 mM NaH¹⁴CO₃, 0.1 mg ml⁻¹ carbonic anhydrase**, with a **1-min** nominal stop time. Those are reproducible proposed starting conditions, not established optimal or saturating conditions for an unseen candidate. The packet can specify prospective saturation and linearity checks, active-site titration, verified full activation, RuBP purity and inhibitor/proteolysis controls, and retain an unevaluable result if any material fails the declared checks. Report activation/yield separately from turnover per active site. A direct radiometric carboxylation method has a documented comparison advantage over the uncorrected coupled readout; any coupled implementation requires enzyme-specific calibration against a direct method before use in the nomination test.

For specificity, a direct competing-gas product assay can use independently calibrated dissolved CO₂/O₂ and resolved PGA/phosphoglycolate standards. Under the stated two-reaction stoichiometry, **vᵒ = vPG** and **vᶜ = (vPGA − vPG)/2**; then **Sᶜ/ᵒ = (vᶜ/vᵒ)·([O₂]/[CO₂])** when the competitive-rate conditions apply. This is a conditional reduction to verify, including blanks, side-product detection and gas equilibration. Total bicarbonate or a growth proxy cannot silently supply the gas ratio or intrinsic specificity. The existing conditional assay can verify arithmetic given admitted rates; it cannot certify the candidate or reference protein.

Independent preparation units, split aliquots, technical repeats, all attempted preparations, common gas/active-site calibration uncertainty and missing/error outcomes need a complete census. Candidate and references should run in blocked/randomized sessions with fixed sample identities and both endpoints retained. The author can freeze proposed n, acceptable uncertainty and a joint decision rule prospectively, explicitly labeled assumptions; published SEM alone cannot determine their power. Eventual organizer acceptance or laboratory adjustment is a post-submission matter and must version any changed protocol. No post-submission measurements or external agreement are required to write this proposed packet, and none is performed by this audit.
