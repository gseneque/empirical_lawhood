## Bounded evaluation opportunity

The defensible immediate task is to decide whether a proposed joint claim has the correct source version, exact construct/assembly, compatible endpoint role and sufficient uncertainty/support, then request only a missing operand that can change that decision. The cases above offer finite, real adverse decisions: superseded rate, rejected factor-two interpretation, incomplete assembly identity, wrong citation, mixed-source vector, distinct Gallionella assemblies, tag/preparation transport, efficiency-as-specificity, missing specificity, unresolved SD/SEM, conflicting source views and derived-as-measured labels. They exercise supply assessment and feedback without inventing a molecular experiment or biological outcome.

This remains a retrospective diagnostic. Freeze the actual roster and complete denominator before the comparison, count abstentions and unresolved cases, and retain each source conflict. Compare against a competent adaptive conventional source audit with the same original documents, numerical checks, acquisition opportunities and budget. Allow ordinary correction, supplier replacement and scientist-assisted interpretation. An equivalent conventional construction counts as a successful comparator. Workflow depth, custody metadata and EL vocabulary alone do not establish a capability advantage.

For a biochemical prediction extension, the direct mutant panel offers six complete mutant-versus-shared-WT contrasts and one missing-specificity contrast. Preserve shared-parent dependence, source study/background hierarchy, native uncertainty conflicts and endpoint-specific missingness. Evaluate both endpoints and unsupported-query abstention separately; growth fitness, model score, calculated oxygenation or ambient efficiency cannot substitute for either target. Source/group-level withholding can test retrospective transport, but cannot turn this exposed panel into prospective validation. Appropriate conventional candidates include no-change/parent, modest sequence ridge/GP, published admissible scores, direct model repair and adaptive acquisition. Molecular work needs a named missing observable and a demonstrated advantage over these cheaper inputs.

## Candidate prospects and stopping boundary

Within the 634-row Flamholz source, no nominal complete row passes the illustrative historical query `vC ≥ 5.46 s⁻¹` and `S ≥ 238`. This is a bounded source observation, not a fixed challenge protocol or proof of biochemical impossibility. The challenge ultimately requires [paired candidate/reference assays](https://millenniumproblems.bio/).

The high-specificity source row for Galdieria records turnover 1.6 s⁻¹, so that illustrative rate comparison requires approximately 3.41-fold improvement while retaining specificity. GWS1B's measured specificity is about 9.10, requiring about 26.2-fold improvement to reach 238 while preserving speed. Neither panel provides a supported mutation map across these extrapolations. A small improvement in either source domain does not substantiate the missing endpoint at the challenge boundary. Selecting a red-algal scaffold is a research direction; an unmeasured residue proposal is not yet a strong nomination.
