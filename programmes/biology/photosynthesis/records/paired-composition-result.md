# Paired composition misses the required joint decision

SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-4.0

The [frozen paired-composition pilot](paired-composition-protocol-v1.md)
completed once on `recchain` on 10 October 2026. **The primary additive model
fails both endpoint criteria.** A stronger predeclared nonlinear conventional
comparator improves aggregate errors on both endpoints, but cannot distinguish
the favourable pS3 specificity response from pST's specificity sacrifice. None
of the three mutation-sensitive methods passes the complete useful-delivery
screen. Close this tested Wilson-to-potato set without changing its methods
after seeing performance.

## Consequence

The source audit has now enabled a real, inexpensive paired-response experiment,
with a negative declared useful-delivery result and an explicitly retained
conventional aggregate improvement. The tested position-blind additive/RBF
set cannot choose the observed joint favourable assembly over the specificity
sacrifice under this protocol. Do not iterate descriptor scales, charge states,
kernels or potato subsets on these exposed labels until a model wins.

A successor needs a scientifically justified site/context-sensitive response
observable and a separate assessment opportunity, including both endpoint roles.
Neither more source wrappers nor candidate search can supply a missing kinetic
relationship. Green-panel predictive value would still require explicit
high-specificity-parent applicability before I95M nomination. A supported
reduced relation remains eligible; new pre-submission laboratory work and a
complete microscopic cycle are not imposed by default. The consequential EL
and exact-nomination requirements of the [programme](programme-contract.md)
remain open, as do Phases 2–9.
