## Consumer question

The candidate-domain aim remains turnover at least equal to maize and specificity at least equal to Galdieria partita in compatible proposed paired assays. These reference comparisons do not silently freeze the illustrative compiled values 5.46/238 as a universal assay protocol. The first assessed task may be narrower known-enzyme contrast discrimination if an adequate exact panel exists, with its relevance and transport limitations declared.

## Current evidence and entry decision

The [native source report](source-panels-report.md) supplies trustworthy source interpretation. The [targeted mechanism report](scientific-supplier-search.md) distinguishes productive kinetics from inhibitor recovery and assembly, and does not supply H3's missing mutation response. The Wilson combination diagnostic has a competent conventional calculation; it is a control for useful inexpensive feedback, not evidence for H3 transport or EL superiority.

An independent stopping audit distinguishes a viable conditional mechanism from a supplied candidate response. In a simple irreversible branch model, commitment rate a and downstream release rate r give kcat = ar/(a+r) and K = (koff+a)r/[kon(a+r)]. Thus kcat/K = kon·a/(koff+a) is independent of r. Accelerating strictly downstream release while retaining the pre-commitment rates can increase turnover without changing conventional specificity; it cannot increase specificity through that cancellation. This is an algebraic consequence of the stated model, not an inferred microscopic rate or mutation effect. Reversible chemistry or a changed commitment requires a newly assessed topology.

The acquired evidence identifies neither a sufficiently large productive-release bottleneck nor an exact red-algal edit that accelerates it. Inhibitor recovery, assembly yield, static structures and fitted WT endpoints do not supply those operands. No new cycle simulation with freely assigned microscopic rates is justified as candidate feedback. The entry stop is falsifiable: admit an exact assay-linked parent/edit, independently constrained productive-step response and an applicable two-endpoint assessment, or select a narrower successor question supported by an adequate mutant panel.

The [reference-pair audit](reference-pair-audit.md) supplies source-bound maize/Galdieria views and a proposed paired-ratio test. Local authoring can freeze proposed control/material/assay assumptions with their uncertainties; eventual organizer acceptance and empirical reference certification remain post-submission requirements. Neither unresolved historical preparation correspondence nor absent new laboratory feedback is silently replaced with a lower target.

### Source-conditioned turnover feasibility bound

For a serial productive cycle with parent time T, step-time fraction f and rate multiplier λ, changing only that step gives T′/T = 1 − f + f/λ. The corresponding gain is 1/[1 − f(1 − 1/λ)]. This assumes additive productive waiting times, 0 ≤ f ≤ 1, and unchanged activation, active-site normalization, remaining steps and branch/commitment probabilities. A reversible network requires its own assessed expression.

Using only the illustrative source point values **1.6 → 5.46 s⁻¹** gives gain R = **273/80 = 3.4125** and the necessary condition f(1 − 1/λ) ≥ **193/273**. Maize 5.46 is a direct radiometric mean; Galdieria 1.6 remains a compilation field with unresolved original metrology. This calculation propagates no measurement uncertainty and defines no universal challenge threshold.

| Hypothetical step acceleration | Necessary parent cycle-time fraction |
|---|---|
| Complete removal | f ≥ 193/273 = **70.69597%** |
| Twice as fast | f ≥ 386/273 = **141.39194%**, outside the allowed fraction |
| Five times as fast | f ≥ 965/1092 = **88.36996%** |
| Ten times as fast | f ≥ 1930/2457 = **78.55108%** |

Once independently supported upper bounds exist, fmax(1 − 1/λmax) < 193/273 would reject that mechanism for this illustrative comparison. The present inputs supply neither f nor an exact edit's λ; no real candidate or biological mechanism has failed this test. The bound concerns turnover. Specificity preservation still requires the separate post-commitment assumptions above and does not establish a mutation response or EL capability.

### Identifiability and the next supplier operand

Steady-state endpoint constants alone cannot identify the release fraction in the schematic irreversible model. For each gas branch i, take positive macro constants ki and Ki, choose any 0 < fi < 1, and set ai = ki/(1−fi), ri = ki/fi and ui = ki/Ki. The branch E + gasi → Xi → Yi → E then retains the same ki, Ki and competing-gas response for every chosen fi. Its specificity is also unchanged. These symbolic countermodels assign no candidate rates and are not fitted mechanisms from the primary literature; they show that a new parameter split cannot be learned from the unchanged macro labels alone.

Conditional on this single product-waiting-state topology, an independently constrained productive-release rate rC would supply fC = kcat,C/rC. The absolute occupancy of that actual committed product-bound state under saturated pure-carboxylation conditions would also supply fC. Either needs exact chemical state, active-site/preparation/activation correspondence and valid kinetics. Actual carboxylation releases two PGA products and may require multiple waiting states; one unresolved product trace or one structural population does not establish the total release-time fraction.

Equilibrium MD occupancy, inhibitor recovery and static near-attack geometry do not supply the required nonequilibrium productive occupancy. A relative barrier might constrain an edit's λ under independently assessed kinetic/prefactor assumptions while still leaving the parent f unidentified. The first supplier entry test is therefore whether authentic evidence constrains the parent productive waiting fraction at all, under an applicable topology. Without it, new production MD/QM would not resolve this selected decision merely by producing a precise observable.

The [exact primary-model assessment](cycle-model-assessment.md) confirms that Witzel's Galdieria column is G. sulfuraria and its catalytic rate combines cleavage with release. Cummins' explicit microscopic formula also supplies no measured G. partita step fraction. These papers cannot furnish the missing scalar through a relabeled fitted parameter; they leave both the biological mechanism and its edit response unresolved.
