# Reopened Rubisco source gap check

SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-4.0

On 11 October 2026, the reopened programme checked public search results for the
specific missing paired turnover and specificity measurements of **Gp SSU I95M
in its candidate background**. The queries were:

- `"Galdieria" "I95M" Rubisco`
- `"Galdieria partita" Rubisco "small subunit" mutant kinetics`
- `"Galdieria" Rubisco "I112M"`

Retrieved results included the original
[G. partita structure paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10336462/), the
[conserved small-subunit mutant study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2781565/),
and a [Chlamydomonas strain carrying Gp small-subunit loops](https://www.chlamycollection.org/product/cc-4743-rbcs1-abefgp-mt/).
The strain record was readable; opening the structure paper returned HTTP 429
and the PMC page returned a browser-check screen. Search also retrieved
structural records and a [2016 technical report](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1330984).

No retrieved result supplied the exact candidate-background paired measurements
needed by the selected assessment. This is a narrow search observation, not an
exhaustive absence claim. Chimeric Chlamydomonas and native Gp preparations cannot
be substituted for one another. Useful further source-backed investigations
remain open.

This contextual check does not add snippets, substituted endpoints or later
exposure to the assessment's admitted 22-file source stock. Its original cutoff,
source digests, complete scenario denominator and unknown actual mutant endpoints
remain intact. Search CPU and latency were not metered and remain unknown.
