## State and observable joins

FM23 and FM23b differ by protonation of **GLU60**; the corrected oxygen MECP
energies are **13.0 versus 7.3 kcal mol⁻¹** in the native table. The 2022 carbon
calculation instead investigates its declared KCX201/His294 proton placements
and uses a different environment and free-energy machinery. An oxygen MECP
energy and a carbon QM/MM free-energy barrier therefore cannot silently become
a paired discrimination observable. Fragment boundaries, constrained atoms,
proton balance, Hamiltonian, entropy, gas standard state and oxygen spin
transmission all matter.

The internal same-FM23 comparison is a useful starting hypothesis. Do not infer
that no matched reference exists merely because the 2019/2022 combination is
incompatible. Conversely, its graph and distance table do not authenticate a
complete runnable pair. A source-supported reconstruction may be assessed as
a new preparation, with its reconstruction choices and uncertainty explicit.
Prior validation of a complete kinetic map is not required before its first
provisional research assessment.

## A model-class falsifier before new sampling

Suppose a common reactive-state occupancy `u` multiplies both low-gas
efficiencies, leaving their branch-specific chemistry and commitment unchanged:

```text
efficiency_C = u × b_C
efficiency_O = u × b_O
specificity = b_C / b_O
```

Changing only `u` cannot change specificity in this model. Likewise, from one
common state with memoryless competing hazards `a_C C`, `a_O O` and a shared
escape hazard, the ratio of carbon and oxygen branching probabilities is
`a_C C / (a_O O)`; the shared escape cancels. These are conditional algebraic
results. They do not exclude conformational regulation in real Rubisco, which
can alter branch-specific chemistry, commitment or reactive ensembles.

An SSU flexibility/gating proposal must therefore supply **two independently
sensitive roles**: productive catalytic progression and relative carbon/oxygen
admission or commitment. Two numerical descriptors of the same common-mode
occupancy do not supply those roles. A simple reduced empirical relation is
eligible if it earns its declared assessment; a complete microscopic cycle is
not mandatory.
