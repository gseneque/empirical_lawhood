## Current objective and scope

**Direction refined 11 October 2026:** the target is simultaneous carboxylation
turnover at least equal to maize and CO2-over-O2 specificity at least equal to
Galdieria partita, established in compatible paired enzyme/reference assays.
The intended benefit pathway is improved carbon-fixation capability. Crop,
ecosystem or climate benefit remains a further empirical question.

EL organises the investigation of the conditions needed to answer and act on
that target: source knowledge, representations, mechanisms, observations,
preparations and qualified use. Established scientific methods are components
when adequate. No EL-versus-conventional tournament, leaderboard or proof of
algorithmic exclusivity is required, scheduled or deferred.

The 10 October decision allowed an honest bounded episode to close without a
positive EL comparison or exact nominee. Preserve that historical meaning and
its completed episodes. It does not make the biological target optional or
completed. A shortlist, supported negative or unevaluable boundary closes its
local claim and leaves an explicit target frontier.

The current computational phase uses public evidence and selected local
resources. No new laboratory feedback, paid service, hardware purchase or
external submission is assumed. Such a needed capability should be identified
as a target requirement, with its actual access and execution conditions. A plan
or software receipt supplies no laboratory result or authority.

## Separate completion levels

| Level | Required evidence and consequence |
|---|---|
| Software conformance | The selected scientific interfaces, exact records and effects behave as specified; no biological qualification follows |
| Scientific episode | A consequential question is independently assessed, with all negative, refused and unevaluable outcomes retained |
| Usable contribution | Procedure adequacy, actual instance supply, consumer admission and joined use are assessed at their own scopes |
| Cumulative capability | The contribution supports a further candidate, question or use; remaining revalidation, acquisition and scope limits are visible |
| Conditional nomination | Exact sequence/assembly, simultaneous rationale, construction proposition and reproducible paired-test plan; the enzyme is still unmeasured |
| Biological target | The same realized candidate meets both target endpoints under compatible paired controls and adequate uncertainty |

A changed ranking, a completed workflow or a justified refusal alone does not
establish new biological knowledge. A useful negative can redirect the target
inquiry; an operational failure without scientific output identifies a recovery
or capability problem. Do not force a nominee or repeatedly query an unsupported
mapping to manufacture progress.

At each wave retain initial stock, the target requirement, alternatives,
scientific assessment, source/exposure and independent units, actual products,
costs and unknowns, attributable human direction, later consequence and remaining
requirements. A prospective redirection preserves the prior verdict and receives
its own scientific identity. Human direction is part of the intended application,
not a failed attempt at full autonomy.

Independent equations, source contrasts, numerical convergence, adverse controls
and actual paired reference assays remain essential. Costs inform feasible work.
Claims of reduced evidence burden or comparative efficiency need their own
supporting evidence if made; no such comparison project is part of this programme.
A conventional method may supply a useful component, with its provenance and
scientific contribution credited correctly.
