This **10 October 2026** design identifies the next useful scientific tests
after the [six-context initial construction](rr-coupled-initial-geometry-protocol-v1.md).
It specifies prospective inference rules, rather than a selected solver,
calculation protocol, fitted kinetic supplier or completed result. All current
structures and biological outcomes are exposed. The immediate scientific task
is to distinguish explanations of the source-domain mutant responses; exact
candidate joint gain and consequential EL capability require later assessment.

The [current decision](redirection-decision-v1.md) corrects two prospective
assumptions: D263N has no measured CKABP processing ratio and cannot be a
labelled unchanged-commitment control; H287's acid-stable hydrolysis numerator
can include terminal misprocessing products such as pyruvate, rather than only
productive PGA. The restricted forward/return interpretation therefore needs
an explicit product/observation premise. A minimum H287 test can omit D263;
separate assay WT anchors remain mandatory if D263 is subsequently included.

## What the initial six contexts can supply

The roster is unedited computational reference / both-LSU D263N / both-LSU
H287N, each under V2/V3 enolate–KCX proton views, from one inherited 9RUB/parent
core. The [inventory](rr-coupled-chemical-inventory.md) conserves the two
physical substrate protons and derives the actual formal charges/electrons.
Reference H287 ND1-H is newly constructed from inherited HIE; modeled H91,
missing native waters and unresolved assay-parent correspondence remain limits.
The initial operation changes coordinates and chemical assignments without
relaxation, gas addition or an admitted embedding Hamiltonian. Its native MM
charges are source witnesses, not a usable reactive-state field.

Consequently these six structures alone supply neither V2/V3 populations,
enolization rates, gas-admission coefficients, productive commitments nor
kcat. A ground-state energy difference would still not supply a barrier or
population without its thermodynamic and kinetic assumptions. A common
geometry failure stops this preparation and motivates a separately selected
matched relaxation; it is no mutant discrimination. Any later chemistry must
admit the complete assigned roster, all exclusions and actual changed-residue
charges before inspecting its calculated response. Neither CO2 nor O2 is in
the current six contexts.

The source foundation is independently checked: the closed parent core has
**44 files / 13,788,149 bytes**, **13,828 atoms / 13,994 bonds**, **7,028 exact
positive-occupancy native joins**, eighteen modeled heavy atoms and sixteen
omitted native source atoms. Its original full archive and topology remain
separately bound external inputs. It is a source packet with zero new
preparations, SCF calls, fits or ArtifactPlane publications, rather than a
portable complete force field. See the [entry](coupled-chemistry-entry.md)
for those bindings and the separate native structure/assay sources.

## Smallest useful reaction test before a joint kinetic map

A finite **local carbon-processing test** can provide useful falsification
without constructing a full catalytic cycle. For each assigned recipe and
proton-view lineage, construct a matched carboxylated intermediate and compare
two explicitly defined exits: return/decarboxylation versus productive forward
processing. Use the retained carbon reaction roles as templates, not the old
unlike fragment/field as an interchangeable Rr calculation. New gas/water,
adduct stereochemistry, H287 charge/proton changes, endpoint basin and proton
identities require their own chemical inventories. Missing 9RUB waters cannot
be filled implicitly. The retained 2022 R/TS/P input runs in the
decarboxylation direction; reverse its interpretation explicitly rather than
calling the existing source a matched forward Rr run.
[Carbon primary mechanism](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.2c01677),
[retained preparation audit](reactive-preparation-result.md).

The minimum discriminating operand is a matched difference between the
forward and reverse activation free energies **from the same carbon adduct**,
with source-compatible rate/transmission assumptions. An adduct energy or
enolate C2 population alone cannot supply it. If only one forward step is
calculated, call it a local progression proxy unless that step reaches a
declared committed productive basin. At least one competing exit must be
represented; assumed irreversibility cannot test the native H287 defect.

For the deliberately restricted two-exclusive-exits, first-pass,
no-recapture model, let their rates be h and d. Then

```text
local forward/reverse odds = h/d
qC = h/(h+d)
log(h/d) = log(Ah/Ad) - (ΔG‡h - ΔG‡d)/(RT)
```

Here qC is a **conditional model quantity**. The native CKABP ratio is
hydrolysis/decarboxylation flux, not this measured probability. Solution decay,
repeated recapture, additional exits and the long product-counting window
would require a different observation model. An unknown Ah/Ad prevents a
unique probability; bound its admitted range independently or retain the
result as unidentified. Do not tune it by genotype to reproduce the ratios.

This test can reject the hypothesis that all assigned contexts maintain
effectively irreversible carbon processing, or show whether actual H287N
chemistry shifts the local competition differently from D263N. A contrary or
preparation-sensitive response is a retained negative result. Agreement is
support for a narrow local mechanism, not a kcat or specificity forecast.
That is a useful minimum scientific task even if a joint supplier remains
unidentified.

## Two-state occupancy is a real alternative to common-mode gating

Distinguish an overall activated/reactive fraction a from redistribution among
two reactive microstates. Let p2+p3=1 within the reactive population, and let
c_s/o_s be productive carbon/oxygen second-order coefficients for state s.
Those coefficients may include separate admission and subsequent commitment:

```text
c_s = kC,s × qC,s ; o_s = kO,s × qO,s
JC = E × a × [CO2] × (p2*c2 + p3*c3)
JO = E × a × [O2]  × (p2*o2 + p3*o3)
Smodel = (JC/JO)*[O2]/[CO2]
       = (p2*c2 + p3*c3)/(p2*o2 + p3*o3)
```

This provisional mapping requires dilute-gas/linear productive flux,
consistent product definitions and applicable state occupations. Overall a
cancels. A change in p does **not** generally cancel: redistribution changes
specificity when the states have different productive discrimination. For
positive o2/o3, define r_s=c_s/o_s; then Smodel is an oxygen-flux-weighted
average of r2/r3 and lies between them. Its derivative with respect to p3 has
the sign of **c3*o2 − c2*o3**. If r2=r3, occupancy redistribution cannot
change specificity. If the independently admitted state-discrimination range
cannot contain the required same-source product-corrected contrast, this
restricted occupancy-only explanation is falsified. Zero/missing oxygen
coefficients require explicit limiting analysis, not division by zero.

This alternative must earn its operands. Relative free energies can give
Boltzmann p only under a justified rapid-equilibrium/common-ensemble model;
exchange barriers or rates must support that limit relative to gas capture
and productive drainage. Otherwise retain the V2↔V3 kinetics and use an
explicit driven state model; its occupations can depend on gas and exits.
Unrelaxed total energies, the reduced C14 tautomer descriptors or arbitrarily
chosen p cannot establish occupancy. A state-independent carbon/oxygen ratio
cannot be made state-dependent merely by renaming the views.

For a constrained occupancy-only competitor, mutation changes independently
computed p, while c_s/o_s remain common across genotypes under a declared
applicability assumption. A more general competitor allows independently
computed changes in state-specific admission/commitment. Keep both: rejecting
strict common-mode gating does not reject the two-state explanation, and
rejecting occupancy-only does not reject coupled chemistry.

The preformed CKABP assay starts from a carbon adduct, rather than the
enolate/gas competition above. Its adduct-state distribution and exchange
must be specified independently if predicted; inserting enolate p into that
experiment without a justified state correspondence does not explain its
hydrolysis/decarboxylation ratio. The same distinction applies to the separate
bulk-detritiation and antagonist-binding assays. A specificity-only success
does not imply delivery of all seven H287 roles.

## Minimum additional operands for a joint response

The local processing test above may precede these operands. A provisional
joint-endpoint assessment requires the following only where its selected
relation actually consumes them:

| Required scientific distinction | Minimum independently supplied operand | Unsupported shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Reactive microstate redistribution | Matched V2/V3 free-energy difference plus evidence for the equilibrium/exchange regime, or explicit exchange rates | Assign convenient populations or infer them from one vertical SCF energy. |
| Carbon versus oxygen productive admission | State-specific relative admission coefficients and commitments, or a directly justified productive discrimination observable | Divide unrelated carbon/oxygen energies, C2 populations or ionization shifts. |
| Carbon processing defect | Matched carbon-adduct forward/reverse competition and its observation assumptions | Force qC=1 because reference carbon addition is favourable. |
| H287 detritiation | An applicable enolization/proton-exchange pathway and bulk-exchange observation model, if claiming that endpoint | Equate no detected bulk exchange with zero elementary abstraction. |
| Productive turnover | Response of a named time-bearing step plus the contribution of other/unsuccessful waits, or another assessed reduced rate relation | Equate a carbon-addition barrier, antagonist exchange or arbitrary f with kcat. |
| D263 activation | Independently admitted activation equilibrium/rate or a clearly conditional fixed-activation comparison | Calculate activation from the already activated structures or infer it from source kcat. |

Reuse retained carbon/oxygen mechanistic roles and compatible primary inputs.
O2 introduces its own spin and crossing/transmission problem; the oxygen
MECP/activation treatment and carbon free-energy barrier are not automatically
commensurate rate coefficients. A future Hamiltonian must supply compatible
standard states, reaction states and environmental treatment before their
ratio is interpreted. [Oxygen primary mechanism](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpcb.9b00518),
[matched-state/source limits](reactive-state-supplier-assessment.md).

Within a matched elementary path, the conditional expression
**k_mut/k_ref = exp(-ΔΔG‡/RT)** requires an unchanged applicable prefactor and
transmission coefficient. It does not identify absolute rates. Carbon versus
oxygen, or hydrolysis versus decarboxylation, may require different physical
prefactors. Any admitted ranges must be set from independent physics/source
evidence before calculated mutant outcomes; genotype-specific label fitting
would remove the test. No prefactor, serial-cycle fraction, missing side-product
fraction or unknown exchange rate is selected in this design.

A complete microscopic cycle is unnecessary for a first reduced assessment.
For a declared serial time model, changing one step by r changes turnover by
**1/(1-f+f/r)** only under that model and a supplied f. Unknown f includes
zero response; failed attempts, commitment and altered bottlenecks can change
the model. Use independently supported time contributions or retain conditional
response bounds. A source-domain directional explanation may be useful before
the data identify an absolute joint rate.

## Prospective assessment and stopping rules

Before a new reaction execution, freeze its finite state roster, chemical
inventories, observables, kinetic/observation mapping, nuisance assumptions,
preparation sensitivities, complete failure denominator and assessment criteria.
This design selects no solver, basis, compute allowance or native execution.
No new numerical success threshold is fitted to the exposed controls here.

Assess separate source-normalized responses: D263 turnover-heavy impairment
with its displayed specificity/activation controls; H287 severe activity loss,
censored detritiation, altered carbon processing and product-profile
discrimination with side products. Report quantitative errors only where
endpoint meaning/normalization permits them. Preserve approximate values as
approximate and censored/absent values as censored/absent. No reported SD means
no statistical confidence claim; unknown metrology is not zero uncertainty.

Propagate independently justified nuisance ranges. A response direction that
changes across those ranges is **UNIDENTIFIED**, not a selected favourable
point. A wrong robust direction falsifies the declared relation at this scope;
unstable geometry/chemical states stop its applicability. If every assigned
case is refused, useful discrimination has not been delivered. Source joins,
inventory conformance and successful calculation are assessed separately from
the predicted biological response.

Compare parent/no-change, common-mode occupancy, two-state occupancy,
irreversible-carbon commitment and actual side-chain chemistry against its
charge-only ablation. Fixed versus consistently relaxed preparations must be
separately declared rather than repaired selectively after seeing responses.
Competent conventional source-aware and scientist-assisted adaptive methods
receive the same tools, structures, solvers, feedback, repair opportunities and
total costs. A conventional success or tie remains a success or tie.

Success on this exposed Rr mechanistic discrimination would supply a narrow
component and a reasoned next experiment. It would not establish an unseen
exact candidate's joint gain, SSU/I95M sensitivity, G. partita transport,
nomination strength or an EL-only constructive capability. Those require a
separate assessed candidate-domain relation and the consequential comparison
in the [programme contract](programme-contract.md). The value of the present
test is delivering or refuting a missing scientific operand, not declaring a
phase complete after another calculation.
