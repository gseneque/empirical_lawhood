## Scientific question

Can an enzyme change improve carbon-fixation turnover while preserving discrimination between carbon dioxide and oxygen?

## Findings and unresolved questions

The programme separates source measurements, conditional kinetic models,
candidate correspondence and the evidence needed for a paired biological test.
Reversible competing-gas calculations leave mathematical possibilities open;
the earlier five-case shortlist missed at least one target for every case.

The retained exploratory nomination selects Galdieria partita small-subunit
I95M. Its [nomination preparation](records/rubisco-nomination-preparation-selection.md)
establishes a conditional test dossier. Actual mutant turnover, specificity,
processing and biological qualification remain unevaluable in that record.
The conditional numerical workflow accepts supplied kinetics; it is not an
edit-to-kinetics predictor or a biological assay.
